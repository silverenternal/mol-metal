## 2. Honest caveat — pocket-invariance pairwise Jaccard = 1.0

> **Mandatory caveat** carried over from `wf_r15_round_re_runs/r12_deflex_allon_10x3/final.md`
> §4.1 and §4.2. The 30 R12 path-A cells return byte-identical
> `n_distinct=20`, `diversity_tanimoto=0.1065`,
> `diversity_homotype=0.0749`, `diversity_subpocket=0.6539` numbers —
> only `reference_tanimoto` varies by pocket (0.093 … 0.229).
>
> **Cause**: `--use-pocket-conditioned-reference` fell back to legacy
> cisplatin seed `[Pt]C#C` for every pocket in the 10-pocket subset
> (`missing_pocket_features` WARN). `--use-learned-prior` and
> `--pocket-boost-strength 2.0` condition *within-pocket* exploration,
> not the seed; since the root is constant, the MCTS converges to the
> same 20 candidates modulo seed RNG.
>
> **Consequence**: pocket-invariance pairwise Jaccard `J(p,q) = 1.000` for
> every (p, q) pair (the candidate set is byte-identical across pockets).
> The within-pocket diversity lift is real (`n_distinct` 1 → 20);
> the across-pocket diversity lift is **NOT demonstrated** by this
> experiment. The 20 candidates share the `[Pt][c]1cn(*)nn1`
> Pt-triazole scaffold family and only the click partner varies.
>
> **Paper §4 framing**: §4 Table 1 row labels are `MEASURED` but the
> caption text (line 432-438 of `04_evaluation.tex`) carries this
> exact pocket-invariance degeneracy caveat. The honest cross-pocket
> diversity claim is deferred to §4.3 (§4.3.4 novel-pocket) and §6
> limitations item (9). §4.3.4 confirms the same byte-identical
> 20-SMILES list on `test_010/011/012` (3 novel pockets), so the
> pocket-invariance is **structural, not a 10-pocket-subset artefact**.
