# ThiolEne connectivity correction — 2026-09-13

The real search witness was reproducible: thiol `Cc1ccc(-c2ccc(S)cc2)cc1` plus alkene `C#CCc1ccc(/C=C/C)cc1` previously produced `C#CCc1ccc(CCC)cc1.Cc1ccc(-c2ccc(S)cc2)cc1`. The products remained disconnected and the alkene gained two hydrogen atoms instead of undergoing thiol addition. The minimal pair `CS` + `C=CC` likewise returned `CCC.CS`; even the non-thiol `CSC` was accepted. These measured before states are in `before.json`.

The old rewrite reduced the first C=C, then searched the entire combined molecule for an arbitrary carbon with cached total valence below four. It did not require the carbon to be a partner alkene endpoint, and it returned a product even when no S–C bond was added. The applicability predicate only required any sulfur atom.

`ThiolEne` now identifies a carbon-bound neutral S–H and a non-aromatic C=C on the second reactant, connects sulfur directly to the selected C=C endpoint, reduces that exact bond, and sanitizes the result. Terminal/less-substituted endpoints are preferred deterministically; newly created stereocenters are left unspecified. Explicit hydrogen atoms are transferred without deletion. The reaction requires unchanged atom count and exactly one fewer connected component, preserving any nonreacting components. The existing hydrogen ledger prevents a reconstructed thiolate from masquerading as a thiol; unrelated pairs without sulfur or C=C return false before RDKit serialization.

The real witness now yields connected `C#CCc1ccc(CC(C)Sc2ccc(-c3ccc(C)cc3)cc2)cc1` (C25H24S), retaining its terminal alkyne. `CS` + propene yields `CCCSC` (C4H10S). `CSC` + propene yields no product. A deliberately supplied nonreacting water component remains present (`CS.O` + propene → `CCCSC.O`); no fragment deletion is used as a repair.

Nine real cases are recorded in `after.json`. Standard representative pairs for CuAAC, SPAAC, Suzuki and AmideCoupling all produce connected main products. The public Suzuki/amide SMARTS encode removal of their declared leaving groups; this patch does not change their treatment. SPC explicitly returns `CCN=PC.N#N`, retaining its legitimate nitrogen byproduct, so no blanket connected-product filter was added to the shared parser. These representative checks do not establish exhaustive correctness of every reaction substrate or regioisomer.

Final validation: **91 tests pass**, including 20 new graph/stoichiometry tests and existing reaction operators, round-10 registry, L4–L6 metrics, synthesis derivations and SynFlowNet adapter tests. Tests verify the new cross-reactant bond joins sulfur to an original alkene endpoint, conservation of all elements including hydrogen, preservation of alkynes/input terms/other components, explicit-H transfer, terminal regioisomer, rejection of thioethers/disulfides/sulfoxides/thiolates and non-alkenes, other four public rules' representative connected products, and SPC byproduct retention.
