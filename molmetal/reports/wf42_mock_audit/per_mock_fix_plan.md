| `annotate_keep_legacy_mock_chemistry` | 1 finding (M22) | Add Wf-43 Phase 3 annotation + cross-reference to Wf-39 real-trajectory bench |
| `annotate_keep_paper_text` | 1 finding (M23) | Add footnote to integration report |
| `annotate_keep_gated_stub_fn` | 1 finding (M18) | No-op — already gated |

The extended audit script:


## §6 Honest-negative caveats

* **The Wf-30 rerun may STILL fail** if meeko vs Pt/Au/Ru atom-type
  mismatch persists for the metal-drug cohort. Per
  `real_tools_inventory.md` §1.1 the Vina 1.2.7 + Meeko 0.8.0
  combination is verified for organic-only ligands; the
  cisplatin/oxaliplatin/auranofin cohort may need a Meeko
  custom-atom-type patch or fall back to the **organic-only cohort**
  (5/10 of the Wf-30 pockets). In that case the rerun produces a
  **real Vina median on 5 pockets** instead of 10, which is still
  better than the stub-floor -9.66 kcal/mol and ships as an honest
  partial-measurement.
* **The 7 KEPT findings are NOT deleted.** They are test scaffolding,
  legacy code, or paper-text pseudo-code. Deleting them would break
  the test suite or the paper contract.
* **The `MEASURED_BUT_STUB` field is preserved** in the audit JSON as
  a historical record. After the §1 rerun, the new
  `vina_kcal_per_mol.json` will have `status: "MEASURED"`; the old
  archive copy (under `molmetal/reports/wf30_dock/archive/`) keeps
  the `MEASURED_BUT_STUB` label for honest-negative traceability.
