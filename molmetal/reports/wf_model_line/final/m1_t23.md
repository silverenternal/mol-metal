- **Why:** `wf_pb_pass_10x3_smoke/final.md` shows 30/30 search-bound at `n_sim=200` (15 no_candidates + 15 seed_only). `wf_pb_pass_real_dock/final.md` shows `pb_pass_rate=1.000` on the 1 cell that DID produce a candidate. ### 3.3 **EV-3: SA production 10×3 sweep at `--sa-weight 0.3` (metal-seed cisplatin arm)** — lifts SA 3.657 → 2.86
