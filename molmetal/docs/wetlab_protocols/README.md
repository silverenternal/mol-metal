## 1. Scope

This directory contains the standard operating procedures (SOPs) for the
three primary wet-lab assays used to validate metallodrug candidates
generated by the Mol-Metal platform:

| Assay | Document | Typical read-out |
|-------|----------|------------------|
| Cytotoxicity (NCI-60 72 h SRB)  | `cytotoxicity_assay.md`      | IC50, GI50 |
| Binding affinity (ITC)          | `binding_affinity_assay.md`  | K_d, ΔH, ΔS, n |
| Cellular uptake (ICP-MS)        | `cellular_uptake_assay.md`   | pg metal / cell, nmol / mg protein |

These templates are aligned with the data schemas expected by the
companion analysis scripts under `molmetal/metrics/`. Any deviation from
the protocol must be documented in the plate/run metadata so that
downstream analyses remain reproducible.

## 2. Facility-wide Safety Requirements

The following rules apply to **every** wet-lab procedure documented in
this directory.

### 2.1 Personal protective equipment (PPE)

- Lab coat (buttoned, long sleeves).
- Nitrile gloves (powder-free); change between samples and immediately
  upon contamination.
- Splash safety goggles (ANSI Z87.1-compliant).
- Closed-toe, liquid-resistant footwear.
- Long hair tied back; no loose jewelry.

### 2.2 Engineering controls

- All work with live cells, opened metallodrug stocks, or biological
  fluids must be performed in a certified Class II Type A2 biosafety
  cabinet.
- Acid digestion and ICP-MS sample prep must be performed in a dedicated
  fume hood with acid-resistant surfaces.
- Cell-culture incubators must be cleaned monthly and CO₂ levels
  calibrated quarterly.

### 2.3 Chemical hygiene

- Store DMSO stocks in a flammable cabinet.
- Quench Pt-, Ru-, and Ir-containing waste per the institutional
  metal-waste SOP (typically alkaline-chloride precipitation, then
  collection as hazardous waste).
- Segregate halogenated and non-halogenated organic waste.
- Maintain a chemical inventory; SDS sheets must be accessible within
  30 s in the lab.

### 2.4 Biological safety

- All cell-line work is performed at BSL-2 containment.
- Liquid biological waste is decontaminated with 10 % bleach (final
  ≥ 0.5 % sodium hypochlorite) for ≥ 30 min prior to disposal.
- Solid biological waste is autoclaved at 121 °C, ≥ 15 psi, ≥ 45 min
  before disposal.

## 3. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and Records

For every assay run:

1. Record **operator initials, plate/run ID, date, instrument ID, and
   lot numbers** of all reagents in the run log (`logs/wetlab_runs.csv`).
2. Confirm QC acceptance criteria from each assay's §9 / §8 before
   accepting the data into the canonical dataset.
3. Upload the raw plate reader / ITC / ICP-MS files to
   `data/wetlab/<assay>/<run_id>/` with checksums.
4. Trigger the corresponding metric job
   (`molmetal/metrics/cytotox_eval.py`,
    `molmetal/metrics/binding_eval.py`,
    `molmetal/metrics/uptake_eval.py`)
   to populate the analysis-ready tables.

## 5. Emergency Procedures

- **Spill of metallodrug solution**: don double gloves and respirator;
  contain with absorbent pads; transfer to hazardous-waste bag; rinse
  the area three times with water; document the incident.
- **Acid splash**: irrigate affected skin / eye for 15 min at the
  nearest eyewash station; seek medical attention; complete an
  incident report.
- **Cell-culture contamination**: decontaminate the cabinet with 10 %
  bleach + 70 % ethanol; quarantine the line; investigate source.
