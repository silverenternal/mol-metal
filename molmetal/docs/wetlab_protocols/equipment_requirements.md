## 1. Purpose

This document specifies the wet-lab equipment required to validate
metallodrug candidates generated by Mol-Metal under the collaboration
framework in `collaborator_outreach_template.md`. It is intended to be
sent to a prospective wet-lab PI alongside the framework document so
that both parties can quickly assess fit before committing to the
Phase-1 acknowledgment.

The requirements are split into **Minimum** (must-have) and **Optional**
(value-adding) tiers.

## 2. Minimum Requirements

The wet-lab facility must have all of the following capabilities
on-site or under direct institutional access (i.e. the assay can be
run within a 4-week window without external contracting).

### 2.1 Cell culture facility (BSL-2)

- Class II Type A2 biosafety cabinet (recertified within the last 12
  months).
- Humidified CO₂ incubator at 37 °C, 5 % CO₂, with quarterly
  temp / CO₂ / RH calibration.
- Inverted phase-contrast microscope (4× and 10× objectives) for
  routine morphology checks.
- Hemocytometer or automated cell counter (e.g. Countess II).
- Mycoplasma testing capability (commercial kit or sent-out service
  with ≤ 2-week turnaround).
- Validated aseptic technique with documented training records for
  every operator.

### 2.2 Cytotoxicity assay capability — SRB (or MTT)

Required to execute the protocol in `cytotoxicity_assay.md`:

- 96-well plate-reading spectrophotometer with **540 nm filter
  (reference 630 nm)**.
- Multichannel pipettes (8- and 12-channel, 10–300 µL).
- Single-channel pipettes (2, 20, 200, 1 000 µL).
- Vortex mixer; benchtop centrifuge with plate rotor.
- Analytical balance (readability 0.1 mg).
- Ultrasonic bath for compound dissolution.
- Reagent stocks: RPMI-1640, FBS (heat-inactivated, lot-tested),
  penicillin–streptomycin, trypsin-EDTA, sulforhodamine B (or MTT),
  TCA, Tris base, DMSO (molecular-biology grade).
- **At least 4 cell lines** authenticated (STR-profiled) within the
  last 12 months. Recommended lines: A549, MCF-7, HeLa, and one
  cisplatin-resistant line (e.g. A549cis or OVTOKO).
- Cisplatin (positive control) in stock.

### 2.3 ICP-MS quantification of Pt / Ru / Ir

Required to execute the protocol in `cellular_uptake_assay.md`:

- ICP-MS instrument (Agilent 7700/7900, PerkinElmer NexION 300/350,
  Thermo iCAP RQ / TQ, or equivalent) tuned for He KED and / or NH₃
  DRC mode to remove polyatomic interferences on ¹⁹⁵Pt, ¹⁰¹Ru, and
  ¹⁹³Ir.
- Microwave digestion system (CEM MARS 6, Anton Paar Multiwave 7000,
  or equivalent) with PFA or quartz vessels.
- Certified fume hood dedicated to acid digestion; argon (and He / NH₃
  if used) gas supply with safe cylinder storage.
- Trace-metal-grade HNO₃ (67–70 %), HCl (32–35 %), H₂O₂ (30 %).
- Single-element Pt, Ru, Ir calibration standards (1 000 µg/mL).
- Internal-standard stock (Y or In, 1 000 µg/mL).
- Metal-free conical tubes (15 mL) and 1.5 mL Eppendorf tubes
  (pre-checked lots).
- Pneumatic pipettes with metal-free tips.
- An operator trained in ICP-MS daily tune and dual-standard
  verification; documented within the last 12 months.

### 2.4 Documentation and QC

- A run log (`logs/wetlab_runs.csv`) that records operator initials,
  plate / run ID, date, instrument ID, and reagent lot numbers.
- An SOP binder (this directory) accessible within 30 s of any
  bench.
- Access to NIST-traceable pH meter standards and a calibrated
  balance.

## 3. Optional Capabilities (Value-Adding)

The following capabilities are **not** required but each one
materially strengthens the wet-lab fit and the resulting manuscript.

### 3.1 Isothermal Titration Calorimetry (ITC)

- For binding-affinity validation (see `binding_affinity_assay.md`).
- Required: MicroCal PEAQ-ITC, NanoITC, or AFFINImeter with a 200 µL
  sample cell.
- Required: ÄKTA / FPLC for protein polishing into ITC buffer.
- Value: provides K_d, ΔH, ΔS, n — a complete thermodynamic profile
  from a single titration.

### 3.2 X-ray crystallography

- For structure determination of metallodrug–protein adducts.
- Required: in-house X-ray source (Rigaku, Bruker) with cryo-stream,
  or institutional beamline access.
- Required: crystallization robot (e.g. Mosquito, Oryx) and
  imaging system.
- Value: atomic-resolution view of the metal coordination geometry
  in the protein pocket; typically converts a Tier-2 paper into a
  Tier-1 journal venue.

### 3.3 NMR spectroscopy

- For speciation studies in solution and stability in cell-culture
  medium.
- Required: 600 MHz (or higher) NMR with cryoprobe and a ¹H/¹³C/¹⁵N
  / ¹⁹⁵Pt (or ¹⁰¹Ru) capability.
- Value: tracks aquation, ligand exchange, and protein binding on
  the chemical timescale.

### 3.4 Hyphenated ICP-MS (SEC-ICP-MS, RP-HPLC-ICP-MS)

- For parent-vs-fragment speciation in cell lysates (see
  `cellular_uptake_assay.md` §11).
- Required: HPLC or FPLC coupled on-line to the ICP-MS.
- Value: distinguishes intact parent complex from fragments /
  protein-bound metal — a question ICP-MS alone cannot answer.

### 3.5 In-house compound synthesis

- For iterative rounds where the dry-lab ranking demands a
  sub-library that was not shipped in Phase 1.
- Required: fume hood, Schlenk line, standard organometallic-glassware
  kit, and a synthetic chemist familiar with Pt / Ru / Ir coordination
  chemistry.
- Value: halves the iteration cycle (no shipping / customs / stability
  concerns).

## 4. Resource Estimates

Rough ballpark estimates for **one round** (Phase 2) of validation of
10 compounds across the minimum-required assays:

| Resource                                  | Quantity                |
|-------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Compounds shipped from Mol-Metal          | 10 × 5–10 mg each       |
| Cell-culture flasks (T75) for 4 lines     | 80 flasks               |
| 96-well plates                            | 60 plates               |
| ICP-MS samples (incl. standards / blanks) | ~ 250 vials             |
| Operator time                             | ~ 320 person-hours       |
| Wall-clock duration                       | 8–12 weeks              |

These figures are conservative; an experienced lab typically compresses
the timeline by 30 %.
