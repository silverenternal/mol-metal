
The 4 tests are organised by honest-mechanism assertion:

- **Section 1 — `test_rh_oversample_correctness`**: Builds a synthetic
  5-metal pool with Rh at low positive rate (matching the Wf-131 P2
  regression pattern: 250 Rh inactive + 50 Rh active + 4 balanced
  metals × 100). Applies `oversample_underrepresented_metals` with
  `underrepresented=["Rh", "Re"], target_pos_rate=0.5`. Verifies:
  (a) Rh positive rate ≥ 0.5 (target reached);
  (b) Ru positive rate unchanged (already-balanced metals passed
  through);
  (c) `stats["Rh"] > 0` (helper appended Rh positives);
  (d) Total Rh row count grew correctly.

- **Section 2 — `test_lomo_cross_validation`**: Builds a synthetic
  5-metal × 30-row pool (150 rows total) and exercises
  `BalancedLOMOSplitter(held_out_metal="Rh", val_fraction=0.1)`.
  Verifies: (a) `train_set.isdisjoint(test_set)`; (b)
  `val_set.isdisjoint(test_set)`; (c) `len(test_set) == n_Rh`; (d)
  `len(train_set) + len(val_set) == n_non_Rh`; (e)
  `0.05 ≤ val_ratio ≤ 0.15` (honours val_fraction=0.1).

- **Section 3 — `test_500_balanced_cohort`**: Loads the Wf-309A Phase
  1 cohort JSON from `wf139_p7_rh_active_cohort/synthetic_rh_pos_neg.json`
  (or its `wf145_p1_rh_active_cohort` mirror). Verifies:
  (a) `stats.n_active == 250`; (b) `stats.n_inactive == 250`;
  (c) `actives` array length 250; (d) `inactives` array length 250;
  (e) `scaffold_overlap_count == 0` (clean scaffold split);
  (f) `catherine_6of6_pass_rate == 1.0` (closure guarantees);
  (g) `load_balanced_rh_smiles` returns 500 (smi, label) pairs with
  balanced counts.

- **Section 4 — `test_m19_metric_lift`**: Constructs the Wf-131 P2
  regression-pattern pool (300 Rh at 16% positive + 4 balanced metals
  × 100). Applies `oversample_underrepresented_metals` and verifies
  the structural lift: (a) `post_pos_rate_Rh > pre_pos_rate_Rh`
  (monotonic lift); (b) `actual_lift ≥ 0.5 × gap` (closes at least
  half the gap to 0.5); (c) `stats["Rh"] > 0` (helper appended Rh
  positives).
