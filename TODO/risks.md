## R9 — Three split labels do not match their implementations (2026-10-19)
`docs/DATA_LEAKAGE_AUDIT.md` (generated by `molmetal/scripts/wf_a51_leakage_audit.py`)
measures no molecule-level leakage anywhere, but three splits claim more separation
than they deliver:

- **CFM v3/v4** — the file is described as a strict Bemis-Murcko split with 0
  scaffold leak. That is true only at the shipped `scaffold_id` key, which is
  stereo-aware and full-complex. Under a desalted stereo-blind ligand key, 73.7 %
  of test rows and 79.6 % of val rows share a scaffold with train. Val clouds are
  what the CFM quality metrics are measured on.
- **MoleculeNet** — `train_moleculenet._bemis_murcko_scaffold` keeps chirality;
  `astartes`, the splitter chemprop moved to, defaults to
  `include_chirality=False`. Our split is therefore easier than the reference,
  and the chemprop comparison is understated by an unmeasured amount.
- **Pt 4-cell** — `load_platinai_4cell_split_table(split_strategy="scaffold_70_10_10")`
  sets `scaffold_id = _stable_hash_int(smiles, 10**8)` and buckets on it: a
  molecule-level hash split. 41.9 % of test rows and 40.8 % of val rows share a
  desalted scaffold with train.

## R11 — m21's split gives test 4.4% of the rows; five of nine cells are under 30 (2026-10-21)

Measured while auditing the A2780cis split on the 4-cell cohort. The same defect
class applies to the NCI-60 9-cell panel, more severely.

- **Cohort is not short of data**: 7,823 labelled rows / 5,207 molecules / 9 cells.
- **Shipped split**: 7,320 / 160 / 343 rows (93.6 / 2.0 / 4.4 %). Per-cell test:
  A2780 48, A2780cis 28, A549 107, DU145 7, HCT116 31, HL60 11, MCF7 90, PC3 18,
  SKOV3 3. SKOV3 also has **0 val rows**.
- **Mechanism**: `_assign_groups` budgets fractions in scaffold counts; the largest
  70 % of the 1,151 scaffolds hold 94.3-100 % of every cell's rows, so test receives
  the leftover singletons.
- **Fix implemented and made the default (2026-10-21)**: `--nci60-split-strategy
  scaffold_coverage_70_10_20` gives 5,371 / 920 / 1,532 rows; every cell >= 25 test
  and >= 22 val; 0 scaffolds span splits; row-order invariant. The loader default is
  now this split; the shipped m21 config, the two probe configs and
  `configs/train_cisplatin_resistance_real_v1.yaml` pin the legacy protocol so old
  artifacts stay reproducible. The pre-flight NCI-60 gate checks both. Artifact:
  `metrics/by_metric/nci60_split_coverage_audit.json`.
- **Still open**: the 9-cell × 5-seed × 50-epoch re-run. Measured cost 7.21 GPU-h on
  the old split, ~5.4 h on the new one. Not launched: GPU0 was carrying the CFM
  session. 0.7294 / 0.5923 stay live with the caveat until it lands; the new number
