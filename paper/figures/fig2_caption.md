## Caption

**Figure 2. End-to-end pipeline -- pocket-conditioned Lambda search.**
The pipeline has two axes: a sequential main spine (top-down) and a
parallel validation band (left-to-right). **Main spine** -- **(a) Pocket
input**: 3D receptor PDB + ligand SMILES; **(b) Lambda MCTS expansion**:
typed-variable substitution + click-rule application + beta-reduction
(`molmetal/molmetal_lam/search_alg/proof_search.py:MCTSProofSearch`);
**(c) MetalGeometryPrior check**: coordination number + geometry
constraint (`molmetal/molmetal_lam/priors/`); **(d) RDKit decode**:
assemble SMILES + sanitise + valence check
(`rdkit.Chem.MolFromSmiles`); **(i) RewardAggregator**: 7-channel score
(qed, sa, binding, novelty, reinvent4, logp, ring_count)
(`molmetal/molmetal_lam/search_alg/proof_search.py:RewardAggregator`).
**Parallel validation branches** -- **(e) QuickVina 2 docking**:
3D pocket-conditioned binding energy
(`molmetal/molmetal_lam/sbdd_env/vina_adapter.py`); **(f) PoseBusters
check**: 22 geometric validity rules
(`molmetal/validation/posebusters_runner.py`); **(g) AdmetAI
multi-property**: drug-likeness + toxicity
(`molmetal/validation/admet_runner.py`); **(h) REINVENT4 multiproperty**:
the `r_reinvent4` channel (newly functional per WF-Extra-2 --
`molmetal/molmetal_lam/sbdd_env/reinvent4_subprocess_adapter.py`). The
dashed arrows show the data fan-out: (d) RDKit decode is the natural
fan-out point, and the four parallel validation channels feed back into
the (i) RewardAggregator on the main spine. Colour bands follow the
input/generation/validation/output taxonomy: blue (input), green
(generation), orange (validation), red (output).
