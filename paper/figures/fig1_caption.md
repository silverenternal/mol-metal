**Figure 1. The 9-layer Molecular Lambda Calculus (MLC) stack.** The
pipeline reads a SMILES (or closed-term) seed and emits a deduplicated
set of valid SMILES. The stack is grouped into four colour-coded phase
bands: **(blue) parsing -- L1 Lexer, L2 Parser, L3 AST Canonicaliser**;
**(green) chemistry -- L4 Click Chemistry Rules, L5 Tile Library, L6
Beta-Reduction Engine**; **(orange) search -- L7 MCTS Search**; and
**(red) output gates -- L8 MetalGeometryPrior, L9 Validity + Dedup
Gate**. Each box shows its layer number, name, one-line description,
input/output types, and the layer's key invariant (top-right).
Vertical arrows denote canonical data flow; the input/output pills on
the far left/right mark the user-visible endpoints. The legend at the
bottom maps fill colour to phase band. The 9 layers correspond to the
9 sections of the per-layer metric catalogue
(`molmetal/reports/lambda_layer_metrics.md`); the per-layer metric
