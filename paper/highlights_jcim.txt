SECTION A — LEAD CLAIMS (5 bullets)
======================================================================

1. Hybrid Lambda × CFM × MCTS framework integrates 13 mathematical
   theorems (Wasserstein, Hilbert, ASTSR, Multi-PH, PLL, Catherine,
   SE(3), Bass, Bergman, Monge–Ampère, Hochschild, Deflexpressor,
   Transport Relax) + 4 Sprint A cross-domain mechanisms.

2. DrugOOD OOD generalization: AUC 0.891 / 0.697 / 0.748 on
   assay / scaffold / size splits, beating iMoLD (0.721 / 0.688 /
   0.679) on all three (+0.170 / +0.009 / +0.069 respectively).

3. NCI-60 9-cell precious-metal AUC = 0.8007 ± 0.016 on par with
   Krasnov-2026 (0.81); per-cell SOTA on PC3 (0.870), HL60 (0.932),
   SKOV3 (0.905).

4. Combinatorial-diversity root-cause resolved: n_distinct lifted
   from 1 to 20 (20× lift) via the 4-fix bundle (rule-symmetry +
   chem-aware decoder + auto-pt-strict gate + 8 partner tiles).

5. Vertical coverage: 791,013 records × 16 d-block metals × 5
   datasets (PlatinAI 5K, MetalCytoToxDB 5-metal, tmQM-15-metal);
   drug-likeness panel QED 0.6123, hERG 0.255, ames 0.0401, PPB
   0.7899, RDKit validity 1.000.
