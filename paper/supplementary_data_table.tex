\subsection*{S11. Data provenance and vertical metric definitions}
\label{supp:s11-data-provenance}

\paragraph{Scope.}
This section exposes (i) the local dataset provenance for the eleven
artefacts inventoried by the Phase-1 metallodrug-vertical audit
(\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_metallodrug\_vertical/phase1\_inventory.md},
2026-09-16, 3\,000-sample random scan per SMILES-bearing dataset), (ii)
the definition of the nine P0 vertical-specific metrics implemented in
\texttt{molmetal/scripts/r4\_lambda\_only\_run.py} (lines
$\approx$1450--1570), and (iii) the cell-line activity-oracle
calibration matrix for the §4.6.1 PlatinAI column.  All numbers below
are either verbatim from the inventory or computed once and frozen; no
value is re-derived at compile time.

\paragraph{Honest framing.}
All eleven rows are \DESIGN{}-with-\texttt{MEASURED}-provenance: the
counts come from the Phase-1 inventory, not from a Round-12/13 sweep.
The MetalCytoToxDB metal-coverage column reports \emph{zero} RDKit
SMARTS hits despite the dataset being multi-metal --- this is a known
parser limitation (ligand split across columns), not a measurement of
absence.  The CSV-column counts in parentheses are the ground-truth
metal breakdown (Ru-dominant photodynamic-therapy complexes).
PlatinAI canonical rate of $\sim$62--66\% is artificially low due to
RDKit kekulisation failures on coordination complexes; the underlying
molecules are not necessarily invalid (see
\texttt{phase1\_inventory.md} \S5 caveat 1).

\paragraph{Honest framing.}
The metric \emph{definitions} are \DESIGN{} (the formulas are
author-fixed); the \emph{smoke numbers} in §5.8
($\text{logp}=-2.40$, $\text{tpsa}=233.5$, $\text{rotb}=9.67$,
$\text{coord}=0$, $\text{cl}=0$, $\text{gsh}=0$, $\text{dna}=0$,

$(pocket, seed, no\,metal\text{-}seed)$ cell per

acceptance number is reported.  The platinum-specific prior
($\text{coord}_{\text{compl}}$ for Pt$^{\text{II}}$ square-planar,
CN$=4$) is enforced separately by
\texttt{metal\_coord\_compliance\_rate} (round 14 workstream).

\paragraph{Honest framing.}
\textbf{PlatinAI is ML-generated; NCI60 + MetalCytoToxDB are wet-lab.}
The PlatinAI oracle returns a probability $\in [0,1]$ trained on
214\,376 internally-labelled SMILES per cell line; it is not a
calibrated wet-lab measurement.  The Pearson $r$ / MAE / RMSE columns
for A2780 and MCF7 are \DESIGN{} pending the MetalCytoToxDB
IC50\textsubscript{Dark} overlay (which intersects PlatinAI on a
small sub-population and requires a SMILES-merge step to overcome the
Counterion+Ligand parser limitation; see
\texttt{phase1\_inventory.md} \S5 caveat 2).  NCI60 is SMILES-less,
so its coverage is reported at the panel level only.  OVCAR-3 is not
a row key in either PlatinAI or MetalCytoToxDB; including it here is
a \emph{forward-looking placeholder} documenting the desired coverage
gap.  All five rows are \DESIGN{} until the §4.6.1 calibration overlay
ships (round-14 workstream; see \texttt{wf\_pivot\_followup/f2\_platinai\_s4.md}
\S3 + \S7 follow-up 4).

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The dataset-provenance table is regenerated by
\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_metallodrug\_vertical/phase1\_inventory.py}
from \texttt{/mnt/storage/data/molmetal/} + the
\texttt{asset\_manifest.json} shipped under the same directory.  The
metric-definition table is a verbatim transcript of the docstrings in
\texttt{molmetal/scripts/r4\_lambda\_only\_run.py:metric\_*}.  The
calibration table is a join of the §2.1 PlatinAI inventory, §2.2
MetalCytoToxDB inventory, and §2.3 NCI60 inventory; no value in this
section is re-derived at compile time.
