\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

We state the limitations of this work explicitly.  Each of the six
items below follows a four-part structure: (i)~a \textbf{diagnosis}
grounded in a \MEASURED{} number from the R17 stack
(\texttt{metrics/by\_metric/*.json}),
(ii)~the \textbf{expected Wf-30/31/32 outcome} tagged
\PROJECTED{} from \texttt{TODO/02\_metrics/METRICS\_TARGETS.md}\S2,
(iii)~an honest statement of \textbf{impact} on the headline claims
and the per-cell-line / per-metal honesty grid of
\S\ref{sec:results:honest}, and (iv)~a forward \textbf{Wf reference}
of the form \texttt{Wf-NN} that names the structural fix and the
matching \S\ref{sec:future} item.  Where a number is
\textbf{PROJECTED} (not yet run on this stack) it is labelled as
such; where a number is \textbf{MEASURED} it carries the explicit
\MEASURED{} tag and the source-report path.  The Tier-1 SOTA anchors
are Krasnov~2026 Ru AUC $0.81$
\citep{krasnov2026metalcytotoxdb} and Rusanov~2025 PlatinAI per-line
AUC $\geq 0.85$ \citep{rusanov2025platinai}.  The cross-cutting audit
underlying this section is consolidated in \texttt{TODO/pending/82\_
metric\_gap\_filling\_plan.md} (G1--G7 gap inventory) and the
post-5-seed Phase A post-mortem in \texttt{molmetal/reports/
wf\_phase\_a\_stage2\_pt/final.md}; per-metric verdicts are tabulated
in the auto-generated \S\ref{sec:limitations:auto} sibling.

  A2780cis AUC $\geq 0.85$ (gate, $+0.202$ over $0.648$,
  cisplatin-resistance axis) on PlatinAI
  \citep{rusanov2025platinai}.  Both gates are \textbf{MEASURED
  failures after the Wf-22 5-seed Phase A retrain}.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{m1 Ru (\MEASURED{} R17 anchor):} $\mathrm{AUC}=
    0.7123 \pm 0.000$ ($n_{\text{seeds}}{=}1$, $n_{\text{train}}{=}797$,
    $n_{\text{test}}{=}135$, scaffold split, plain D-MPNN multi-task,
    5 epochs; \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/metalcytotox\_ru\_auc.json},
    $\Delta_{\text{Krasnov}}{=}{-}0.098$, $\Delta_{\text{gate}}{=}

    $\mathrm{AUC}=0.546 \pm 0.003$ after Wf-22 5-seed retrain

  \item \textbf{Phase A BLOCK: 5-seed Pt retrain shipped end-to-end
  ($79$~min Blackwell wall-clock) but did \emph{not} lift AUC.}
  \label{sec:limitations:phase-a-block}
  The Phase A plan \texttt{TODO/03\_plans/R17\_AUC\_RECOVERY\_PLAN.md}
  committed to $30$ min wall-clock per ultracode with two concurrent
  ultracodes ($60$ min total concurrent budget).  \MEASURED{} actual
  outcome per \texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_phase\_a\_stage2\_pt/final.md}
  and \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/platinai\_3cell\_resistance.json}:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Ru 5-metal multitask} (\texttt{wf\_phase\_a\_stage1\_ru})
    died at host contention --- Stage~1.4 \texttt{train\_ru\_multitask.py}
    stopped after $7$ log lines
    (\texttt{logs/train\_ru\_multitask/seed\_all.log}); tmQM encoder
    arch mismatch (V4MultiTaskDMPNN \texttt{DirectedMPNN}, $36$ tensors,
    hidden=$300$) incompatible with baseline ckpt (MetalCytotoxModel,
    $12$ tensors, hidden=$128$, \texttt{\_GRUUpdate}); fell back to
    random init and was killed before epoch~$0$ forward pass.
    \item \textbf{Pt 3-cell resistance retrain} \textbf{ran to
    completion} on Blackwell single ultracode in $79$ min (no
    concurrent job) --- \MEASURED{} A2780cis $\Delta = -0.018$
    ($0.648 \to 0.630$), MCF-7 $\Delta = -0.081$ ($0.584 \to 0.503$),
    A549 $\Delta = -0.125$ ($0.630 \to 0.505$), 3-cell mean collapsed
    to $0.546 \pm 0.003$.  The combined expected lift
    ($+0.05$ tmQM + $+0.02$ focal + $+0.04$ side-feat $\approx +0.11$)
    did \textbf{NOT} materialise.
  \end{itemize}
  \textbf{R18 structural-fix protocol (\PROJECTED{} Wf-31):} the
  Phase A BLOCK verdict is empirically \emph{re-opened}, not just
  \emph{re-acknowledged}.  Wf-31 re-attempts both runs on the
  Blackwell + RTX 5090 dual-GPU host
  (\texttt{TODO/pending/82\_metric\_gap\_filling\_plan.md}\S2
  Phase C): Blackwell \texttt{gpu0} handles the SE(3) SB
  5-metal multitask ($\texttt{train\_ru\_5metal\_se3sb.py}$);
  RTX 5090 \texttt{gpu1} handles the cisplatin-resistance v2
  ($\texttt{train\_cisplatin\_resistance\_v2.py}$); 5 seeds
  $\times$ 50 epochs each, expected wall-clock $\sim 8$~h per run,
  total concurrent budget $\sim 8$~h (vs.\ $60$~min available to
  Phase A).  The dual-GPU Blackwell + RTX 5090 host lifts the
  per-ultracode wall-clock from $79$~min to a projected
  $\sim 8$~h envelope \emph{but} the host contention that killed
  the Wf-22 Phase A attempt is structurally avoided by the
  dual-GPU split.
  \textbf{Impact:} no Tier-1 AUC lift is attributable to a Phase A
  fix in this round.  The $0.7123$ (m1, 1-seed) and $0.546$ (m2
  3-cell mean, 5-seed) numbers in \S\ref{sec:limitations:tier1-auc}
  are the empirical anchors until Wf-31 ships.  The $79$-min Pt
  wall-clock is at least \textbf{\$success 1}: Blackwell completed
  the 5-seed retrain end-to-end (no NaN, no OOM, all 5 per-seed
  test AUC JSONs written); the runtime envelope is within the
  cumulative Blackwell per-ultracode budget when run single-ultracode
  but is $2.6\times$ over the strict $30$-min/ultracode CI budget
  (see \S\ref{sec:limitations:ci-budget} below).
  \textbf{Wf reference:} \textbf{Wf-31} (Phase C, \texttt{molmetal/
  reports/wf31\_*/final.md}, target ship 2026-10-03 per TODO-82 \S4
  timeline) and \textbf{Wf-29} (Phase A se3sb / transport / multiph

  % ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  % §6.3 — Blackwell CI 30-min budget (CI framework, not GPU)
  % ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  \item \textbf{Blackwell CI $30$-min budget is a CI framework
  constraint, not the local RTX 5090 hardware envelope.}
  \label{sec:limitations:ci-budget}
  The ``$30$-min/ultracode, $60$-min concurrent'' budget cited in
  Phase A \texttt{TODO/03\_plans/R17\_AUC\_RECOVERY\_PLAN.md} is the
  \textbf{GitHub Actions Blackwell runner timeout}, not the local
  RTX 5090 hardware ceiling.  The distinction matters because:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{CI runner (Blackwell):} $30$ min single-ultracode
    timeout per job; $60$ min total concurrent budget across the
    two ultracodes; this is the \emph{structural} gate for any
    GPU-retrain run that must terminate inside one CI cycle.
    \item \textbf{Local RTX 5090 (\$experimental):} no hard timeout;
    Wf-22 Pt $5$-seed retrain ran $79$ min wall-clock on Blackwell
    single ultracode; Wf-31 Phase C expects $\sim 8$~h per run on
    the dual-GPU Blackwell + RTX 5090 host, well outside the CI
    $30$-min envelope but acceptable as an offline \emph{local}
    run that ships its JSON outputs to the CI cache for downstream
    paper-build consumption.
  \end{itemize}
  \MEASURED{} evidence: \texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_phase\_a\_stage1\_ru/
  final.md} shows the Ru run died at $7$-line init, not at the CI
  timeout --- the contention was on the single Blackwell device
  (gfx1101 wave64) being shared by \texttt{train\_ru\_multitask}
  and \texttt{train\_cisplat\_resistance\_3cell}, both attempting
  to acquire the same ROCm context.  The Wf-22
  \texttt{train\_cisplat\_resistance\_3cell} reported $104\%$ CPU
  usage while \texttt{train\_ru\_multitask} was starved after
  init line $1$--$7$.  The CI runner timeout did not fire; the
  GPU context contention killed the lower-priority job first.
  \textbf{R18 fix (\PROJECTED{} Wf-29 + Wf-31):} the dual-GPU
  Blackwell + RTX 5090 split avoids the contention by assigning
  one job per device, and the Wf-29 Phase A smoke benchmark
  validates the split before Wf-31 Phase C commits its
  $\sim 8$~h wall-clock.  CI runs of Wf-31 deliverables will use
  a ``split + checkpoint + resume'' wrapper
  (\texttt{molmetal/scripts/wf31\_ci\_split.py}, planned in
  TODO-82 \S3 NEW skill \texttt{mol-metal-gpu-execution}) so the
  CI $30$-min budget is respected per CI step while the local
  run continues to completion.
  \textbf{Impact:} the Wf-22 5-seed Pt retrain (\$success 1) is
  reproducible \emph{locally} on the same Blackwell single-ultracode
  envelope, but the CI framework cannot host it directly without
  the split + checkpoint wrapper.  This is a CI infrastructure
  limitation, not a hardware limitation; the Blackwell + RTX 5090
  host is $2.6\times$ the CI per-ultracode budget but is the
  correct experimental envelope for R18 close-out.
  \textbf{Wf reference:} \textbf{Wf-29} (Phase A se3sb / transport
  / multiph smoke benchmark, validates dual-GPU split before
  Wf-31) and \textbf{Wf-31} (Phase C, depends on the Wf-29 smoke).

  \item \textbf{Vina Dock $m_3$ median $-1.2885$~kcal/mol
  ($n{=}100$, single-pocket $830$c) \MEASURED{} below TransDiffSBDD
  $-8.5$~kcal/mol gate.}
  \label{sec:limitations:vina-dock}
  The CFM pipeline headline reports a \MEASURED{}
  $\mathrm{median\_vina}=-1.2885$~kcal/mol on the R17 single-pocket
  $830$c smoke ($100$ Lambda click products $\times$ AutoDock
  Vina $1.2.7$, exhaustiveness=$8$, $n_{\text{poses}}{=}5$,
  $\mathrm{mean}=-1.3546 \pm 0.1891$, $\mathrm{min}=-1.82$,
  $\mathrm{max}=-1.13$; \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/vina\_score.json}).
  The TransDiffSBDD \citep{transdiffsbdd2025} CrossDocked median
  target is $\leq -8.5$~kcal/mol (10-pocket panel, $100$
  candidates $\times$ $10$ pockets); the local $830$c measurement
  is $+7.2$~kcal/mol above the SOTA gate.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Single-pocket limitation (\MEASURED{} R17):} the
    $830$c pocket is an MMP13 collagenase receptor (positive control
    for binding-affinity regression) --- it is \emph{not} a CrossDocked
    test pocket, so the $-1.2885$~kcal/mol number is a pipeline
    smoke test, not a TransDiffSBDD-class comparison.
    \item \textbf{Strict decode-ratio collapse (\MEASURED{} R17):}
    the CFM strict-contract measurement is \MEASURED{}
    $\mathrm{decode\_ratio\_rdkit\_strict}=0.5$ on synthetic $10$-atom
    Pt-click clouds (Path~B, $n{=}192$, overvalent atoms fail
    sanitisation) and $0/8$ on the $830$c pocket
    ($500$-step CPU/GPU smoke); the lenient-contract
    $\mathrm{decode\_ratio}=1.000$ is consistent across $3$ seeds
    $\times$ $10$ probes at the R17 10K-step retrain
    (\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_r17\_10k\_retrain\_done/}) but is
    an atom-only check, not a sanitisation check.
    \item \textbf{$\mathrm{mean\_atoms}=8.0$ unconditional-mode
    collapse (\MEASURED{} R17):} every probe step emits exactly
    $8$ atoms regardless of pocket conditioning --- the $8$-atom
    constant is the unconditional-mode mean, indicating the ODE
    path runs but the model has \emph{not} learned
    pocket-conditioned placement.
    \item \textbf{R18 expected lift (\PROJECTED{} Wf-30):} $10$
    CrossDocked pockets $\times$ $5$ candidates $\times$ $5$ seeds
    = $250$ docking evaluations via the new
    \texttt{molmetal/scripts/dock\_10pockets.py} + SE(3) SB
    5-seed $\times$ 50-epoch retrain; expected median
    $\leq -8.5$~kcal/mol (TransDiffSBDD tier) on the $10$-pocket
    panel (TODO-82 \S2 Phase B, target ship 2026-10-01).
  \end{itemize}
  \textbf{Impact:} the geometric-SBDD headline gap of
$\sim 2$~kcal/mol Vina \emph{cannot} be closed on a single-pocket
$830$c measurement; the $-1.2885$~kcal/mol anchor is a pipeline
smoke, not a SOTA comparison.  No CFM-side cell of
Table~\ref{tab:main_v3_template} is eligible for
$\DESIGN{}\!\to\!\MEASURED{}$ promotion under the strict contract
until Wf-30 ships.  The hybrid Lambda$\times$CFM column of
Table~\ref{tab:sbdd-comparison} is \DESIGN{} pending the Wf-30
production panel.
  \textbf{Wf reference:} \textbf{Wf-30} (Phase B, $10$ pockets
  $\times$ $5$ seeds Vina retrain, \texttt{molmetal/reports/
  wf30\_*/final.md}, target ship 2026-10-01 per TODO-82 \S4
  timeline); Wf-30 cascades to m4 Success Rate and m10 Vina Min
  in Table~\ref{tab:main_v3_template}.

  \item \textbf{Affinity $m_{16}$ Pearson $r=0.207$ \MEASURED{},
  seed-fragile ($0.005 / 0.519 / 0.071$ spread).}
  \label{sec:limitations:affinity}
  The pIC$_{50}$ activity predictor returns \MEASURED{}
  $\mathrm{Pearson}\,r=0.207$ on the HeLa48h/dark cohort test split
  ($\sim 700$ test rows, $3$ seeds $\times$ $50$ epochs, margin=$0.5$
  best of $0.25$ / $0.5$ / $1.0$ sweep; \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/
  pearson\_r\_pic50.json}).  The seed-fragile per-seed values are
  $0.005$ (seed $0$), $0.519$ (seed $1$), $0.071$ (seed $2$) --- a
  $\sigma = 0.228$ spread that puts the $r=0.207$ headline within
  one std of the no-signal floor.  The Boltz-2 \citep{boltz2024}
  reference is $\mathrm{Pearson}\,r \approx 0.86$ on the same
  affinity regression task; the local measurement is $-0.65$ below
  the Boltz-2 anchor.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Seed-fragility (\MEASURED{} R17):} the
    $0.005 / 0.519 / 0.071$ per-seed spread (range $0.514$)
    dominates the $0.207$ headline; the bound-aware accuracy
    $\mathrm{ba\_acc}=1.0$ at margin$\geq 0.5$ is the only stable
    signal in the predictor.
    \item \textbf{Ridge baseline (\MEASURED{} R17):} the ridge
    regressor on the same split returns \MEASURED{}
    $\mathrm{Pearson}\,r=0.572$, beating the D-MPNN neural predictor
    by $+0.365$ on the same scaffold split
    (\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_extra1\_full/final.md}).
    \item \textbf{R18 expected lift (\PROJECTED{} Wf-32):} the
    new \texttt{molmetal/scripts/train\_affinity\_regression.py}
    on RTX 5090, PlatinAI 4-cell + MetalCytoToxDB 5-metal
    affinity cohort, $5$ seeds $\times$ $50$ epochs, expected
    $\mathrm{Pearson}\,r \geq 0.50$ (more realistic target than
    Boltz-2 $0.86$ on a small training set; TODO-82 \S2 Phase D,
    \S6 acceptance criteria).
  \end{itemize}
  \textbf{Impact:} the pIC$_{50}$ predictor does \emph{not} beat
  the ridge baseline; the $0.207$ headline is the honest-negative
  anchor for the regression-on-fixed-molecule baseline.  No
  in-silico phenotype claim in \S\ref{sec:results:honest} relies
  on the pIC$_{50}$ predictor --- the metallodrug-vertical
  activity ranking uses the Krasnov Ru AUC + Rusanov PlatinAI
  AUC + NCI-60 9-cell AUC as the load-bearing evidence, and the
  $m_{16}$ regression is reported as a pipeline smoke test, not
  as a phenotype oracle.
  \textbf{Wf reference:} \textbf{Wf-32} (Phase D, $m_{16}$
  affinity regression retrain, \texttt{molmetal/reports/wf32\_*/
  final.md}, target ship 2026-10-05 per TODO-82 \S4 timeline).
