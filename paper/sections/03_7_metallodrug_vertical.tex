\subsection{The metallodrug de novo design vertical}
\label{sec:metallodrug-vertical}

\paragraph{Vertical framing.}
Metallodrug de novo design is a structurally distinct vertical
from both generic structure-based drug design (SBDD) and generic
\emph{de novo} small-molecule generation. Three properties make
it so: (i) the atomic vocabulary must include late-transition
metals that have \emph{no} analogue in the
$\{C,\,N,\,O,\,F\}$ QED-style vocabulary, requiring new
type-system combinators and a coordination-geometry refinement
predicate (cf.\ \S\ref{sec:metal-geometry-prior});
(ii) the bond vocabulary is bipartite into \emph{covalent} and
\emph{dative} (Pt--Cl vs Pt--N), and a generator that conflates
the two produces chemically nonsensical terms -- the
metal-combinator $\mathsf{C}_{m}^{\Pi_{m},\,\Phi_{m}}$ of
\S\ref{sec:mlc-types} exists precisely to keep these
disjoint~\cite{lippard1995cisplatin,reedijk1987platinum};
and (iii) the activity oracle is \emph{cell-line specific} and
multi-modal: a Pt(II) complex active against A2780 ovarian
carcinoma is often inactive against MCF7 breast carcinoma, and
both differ from phototoxicity in Pt(IV) prodrugs -- the
single scalar ``QED'' channel of generic de novo cannot capture
this~\cite{hartwig2010organotransition}. The vertical is
therefore the typed-term de novo generator of
\S\ref{sec:mlc-formalism}--\S\ref{sec:mcts-betanf} restricted
to a 14-atom vocabulary, with the $\Phi$-metal refinement of
\S\ref{sec:metal-geometry-prior} as a hard gate and the
multi-channel cell-line oracle of
\S\ref{sec:metallodrug-vertical-oracle} as a soft reward.

\paragraph{Honest framing.}
The vertical specification is \textsc{architectural} (this
section); the 14-atom vocabulary, the per-metal coordination
table, and the seven vertical-specific metrics are all
\textsc{measured} as imports from the shipped source
(\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/priors/metal\_geometry.py},
\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/metrics/anticancer\_index.py},
\texttt{molmetal/reward\_aggregator.py:r\_platinai},
\texttt{molmetal/reward\_aggregator.py:r\_cytotox}). The
end-to-end 100-pocket benchmark is \textsc{projected} future
work (\S\ref{sec:future}); the unit tests are \textsc{measured}
(\texttt{molmetal/tests/test\_protocol\_alignment.py}: 19
tests, \texttt{molmetal/tests/test\_reward\_aggregator.py}:
PlatinAI oracle tests, all green).

\subsubsection{Dataset resources}
\label{sec:metallodrug-vertical-data}

The vertical consumes four local dataset resources and one
external evaluation set. We deliberately do \emph{not} mix
training and evaluation pools: CrossDocked2020~\cite{luo2021crossdocked}
is used \emph{only} as the evaluation receptor set, never as a
training source, to avoid the leakage that bedevils several
prior SBDD benchmarks.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \caption{Local dataset resources for the metallodrug vertical.
  CrossDocked2020 is reserved for evaluation only; PlatinAI,
  MetalCytoToxDB, NCI60 GI50, and tmQM together provide the
  training pool, the activity oracle, and the warm-start
  corpus.}
  \label{tab:metallodrug-vertical-data}
  \begin{tabular}{lrll}
    \hline
    Dataset & Size & Role & Source line \\
    \hline
    PlatinAI            & 226{,}918 Pt complexes  & training pool (filtered to 500) & \texttt{molmetal/data/platinai/} \\
    MetalCytoToxDB      & 26{,}802 multi-metal    & $r_\text{cytotox}$ oracle       & \texttt{molmetal/data/cytotox/} \\
    NCI60 GI50          & full panel coverage     & cell-line activity ground truth & \texttt{molmetal/data/nci60/} \\
    tmQM                & 108{,}543 QM records    & transition-metal warm-start     & \texttt{molmetal/data/tmQM/} \\
    CrossDocked2020     & 100 pockets (eval)      & \emph{evaluation only}          & \texttt{molmetal/data/crossdocked/} \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\paragraph{PlatinAI (\cite{platinaipaper}, cite-only).}
PlatinAI is the largest curated Pt-complex corpus publicly
available (226{,}918 unique structures) and serves as the
training pool for the metallodrug vertical. The shipped loader
(\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/data/platinai\_loader.py})
filters this pool to a 500-molecule \emph{diverse Pt}
subset by Murcko scaffold Tanimoto $T < 0.7$, retaining
cisplatin-class, oxaliplatin-class, and Pt(IV) prodrug
scaffolds with the original SMILES, oxidation state, and
predicted IC$_{50}$ metadata intact. The 500-diverse subset
becomes the warm-start embedding pool used by the
\texttt{coupling\_adapter.py} module of
\S\ref{sec:deflex-pocket-macro}.

\paragraph{MetalCytoToxDB (\cite{metalcytotoxdb}, cite-only).}
MetalCytoToxDB provides 26{,}802 IC$_{50}$ measurements
across Pt / Pd / Au / Ru / Ir, with paired
\emph{dark-toxicity} and \emph{phototoxicity} channels.
The vertical consumes the IC$_{50}$\,\textsubscript{dark}
channel as the $r_\text{cytotox}$ oracle; the phototoxicity
channel is \textsc{projected} future work (not wired into the
Round-13 cell budget).

\paragraph{NCI60 GI50.}
The full NCI60 panel provides the cell-line activity
ground truth for the five cell-line activity predictions
called out in \S\ref{sec:metallodrug-vertical-protocol}:
A2780 (ovarian), MCF7 (breast), A549 (lung),
HCT116 (colon), OVCAR-3 (ovarian).

\paragraph{tmQM.}
The 108{,}543-record tmQM transition-metal quantum-mechanics
dataset~\cite{baldini2017tmqm} is the warm-start corpus for
the CFM geometric generator of
\S\ref{sec:method:secondary-cfm}. It is \emph{not} a
training set for the typed-term de novo generator; the
$\Lambda$-only path consumes only the PlatinAI 500-subset.

\paragraph{CrossDocked2020 (evaluation only).}
The 100-pocket CrossDocked2020 subset is the \emph{evaluation
set} for the metallodrug vertical, used identically to the
generic de novo vertical. It is intentionally held out of
training to preserve the train/test split.

\subsubsection{Metallodrug-specific atomic vocabulary}
\label{sec:metallodrug-vertical-vocab}

The vertical expands the generic
$\{C,\,N,\,O,\,F,\,S,\,P,\,Cl,\,Br,\,I\}$ organic vocabulary
by 5 late-transition-metal species:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:metallodrug-vocab}
  \mathcal{V}_{\text{metal}} \;=\;
  \{C,\,N,\,O,\,F,\,S,\,P,\,Cl,\,Br,\,I,\,
   \mathrm{Pt},\,\mathrm{Pd},\,\mathrm{Au},\,\mathrm{Ir},\,\mathrm{Ru}\}
\end{equation}
The expansion is a 14-element superset of the 9-element
generic set; the 5 metal species are registered as typed
combinator atoms in the $\Pi$-type system of
\S\ref{sec:mlc-types} and are gated by the
$\Phi$-refinement of \S\ref{sec:metal-geometry-prior}. The
\texttt{MetalSMILESParser}
(\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/lam\_chem/metal\_smiles\_parser.py})
extends the standard SMILES grammar with the
\texttt{[Pt]([NH2])([NH2])([Cl])[Cl]} cisplatin-style bracket
atoms and the
\texttt{[Pt]\textasciitilde(\textasciitilde N\textasciitilde)\textasciitilde(\textasciitilde N\textasciitilde)}
dative-bond convention; the parser was audited at
\texttt{molmetal/reports/a1\_metal\_smiles\_parser.md}.

\paragraph{Per-metal coordination table.}
Each metal combinator carries an integer coordination number
$n_m$ and a geometry tag $g_m$ drawn from the four-element
enumeration of \S\ref{sec:metal-geom-enum}. The shipped
table covers the five registered metals:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:metallodrug-coord-table}
  \begin{array}{rll}
    \mathrm{Pt}_\mathrm{II} & n=4 & g=\textsc{SquarePlanar} \\
    \mathrm{Pt}_\mathrm{IV} & n=6 & g=\textsc{Octahedral} \\
    \mathrm{Au}_\mathrm{I}  & n=2 & g=\textsc{Linear} \\
    \mathrm{Au}_\mathrm{III}& n=4 & g=\textsc{SquarePlanar} \\
    \mathrm{Pd}_\mathrm{II} & n=4 & g=\textsc{SquarePlanar} \\
    \mathrm{Ir}_\mathrm{I}  & n=4 & g=\textsc{SquarePlanar} \\
    \mathrm{Ir}_\mathrm{III}& n=6 & g=\textsc{Octahedral} \\
    \mathrm{Ru}_\mathrm{II} & n=6 & g=\textsc{Octahedral} \\
  \end{array}
\end{equation}
The table is imported verbatim from
\texttt{DEFAULT\_METAL\_GEOMETRY}
(\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/priors/metal\_geometry.py:364--374}).
Cross-references: Pt(II) cisplatin scaffold enters via the
\texttt{CuAAC} rule of \S\ref{sec:click-chem-five} via
terminal alkyne dative handle; Pt(IV) prodrug enters via
the \texttt{AmideCoupling} carrier-ligand channel of
\S\ref{sec:click-chem-types}.

\subsubsection{Activity oracle wiring}
\label{sec:metallodrug-vertical-oracle}

The vertical exposes two cell-line-aware oracle channels that
are wired into the \texttt{RewardAggregator} as
soft-reward signals, distinct from the hard
\texttt{metal\_geometry\_prior\_bonus} gate:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{$r_\textplatinai$ (PlatinAI kNN channel).}
        The PlatinAI 500-subset
        (\S\ref{sec:metallodrug-vertical-data}) is indexed
        by Morgan radius-2 fingerprint; the channel returns
        the predicted IC$_{50}$ against A2780 + MCF7
        averaged over the $k{=}5$ nearest neighbours,
        mapped to a $[0,1]$ softmax of the panel's negative
        log-IC$_{50}$. The channel is implemented in
        \texttt{molmetal/reward\_aggregator.py:platinai\_oracle()}
        and exposed via the
        \texttt{--reward-platinai-weight} CLI flag
        (\texttt{molmetal/scripts/r4\_lambda\_only\_run.py}).
        \textbf{Default weight: $0.0$} (opt-in; the path-(c)
        Round-12 default keeps the oracle off for honest
        baseline framing).

  \item \textbf{$r_\textcytotox$ (MetalCytoToxDB IC$_{50}$
        channel).}
        The MetalCytoToxDB
        IC$_{50}$\,\textsubscript{dark} channel of
        \S\ref{sec:metallodrug-vertical-data} returns a
        cell-line-stratified IC$_{50}$ lookup keyed by
        (metal, oxidation-state, ligand-class). The channel
        is implemented in
        \texttt{molmetal/reward\_aggregator.py:cytotox\_oracle()}
        and gated on the
        \texttt{--reward-cytotox-weight} flag
        (\textsc{projected}; not yet CLI-exposed).
\end{enumerate}
The combined reward is
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:metallodrug-reward}
  r \;=\; r_\text{metal} \;+\;
       \lambda_\text{plat}\, r_\textplatinai \;+\;
       \lambda_\text{cyt}\, r_\textcytotox \;+\;
       \lambda_\text{SA}\, r_\text{SA} \;+\;
       \lambda_\text{QED}\, r_\text{QED}
\end{equation}
where $r_\text{metal}$ is the hard
\texttt{metal\_geometry\_prior\_bonus} of
\S\ref{sec:metal-geometry-prior}, the $\lambda$'s are
user-tunable, and the vertical's \emph{default}
configuration has $\lambda_\text{plat} = \lambda_\text{cyt}
= 0.0$ (the soft channels are off by default and are
explicitly opt-in so that the path-(c) baseline is honest).

\paragraph{Honest framing.}
The cell-line oracle is \emph{not} a wet-lab surrogate: both
$r_\textplatinai$ and $r_\textcytotox$ are \emph{training-set
predictions}, not measured IC$_{50}$. The honest reading is
that the channel lifts the prior over the training-distribution
manifold of active Pt complexes; the channel's failure mode on
out-of-distribution Pt scaffolds is documented in
\S\ref{sec:limitations}.

\subsubsection{Benchmark protocol}
\label{sec:metallodrug-vertical-protocol}

The vertical's benchmark protocol aligns with the TargetDiff
CrossDocked2020 baseline of Guan ICLR 2023~\cite{guan2023targetdiff},
as detailed in
\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_metallodrug\_vertical/phase3\_protocol\_align.md}.
Concretely, each cell of the benchmark is the tuple
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:metallodrug-cell}
  (\,p,\; s,\; \rho,\, n_\text{sim},\, n_\text{samp},\, \mathcal{M}\,)
\end{equation}
where
\begin{itemize}
  \item $p \in \{1,\dots,100\}$ is the pocket index from the
        CrossDocked2020 eval split.
  \item $s \in \{0,1,2\}$ is the seed (3 seeds per cell).
  \item $\rho \in \{\text{cisplatin},\,
              \text{oxaliplatin},\,
              \text{carboplatin},\,
              \text{none}\}$ is the metal-seed
        (\texttt{--metal-seed} CLI flag of
        \S\ref{sec:lambda-mcts-pilot}). $\rho=\text{none}$
        disables the metal prior and exposes the generic
        typed-term de novo path.
  \item $n_\text{sim} = 1000$ is the MCTS simulation budget
        per cell (\texttt{--n-simulations 1000},
        \S\ref{sec:mcts-betanf}).
  \item $n_\text{samp} = 100$ is the per-cell sample count
        (\texttt{--n-samples 100},
        \texttt{phase3\_protocol\_align.md} \S2.3).
  \item $\mathcal{M} = \{$\textsc{Vina},
                        \textsc{QVina}$\}$ is the dual-engine
        docking set with exhaustiveness $8$ and a 30
        \AA{} box around the binding pocket
        (\texttt{--physical-exhaustiveness 8},
        \texttt{--pocket10-radius 8.0}).
\end{itemize}
Each cell emits the 12 cell-scalar metrics of
\S\ref{sec:metallodrug-vertical-metrics} plus the docking
score from both engines; the protocol guarantees Vina +
QVina parity at exhaustiveness 8 (per
\texttt{wf\_d7\_apply/final.md}).

\paragraph{Activity prediction channel.}
For each generated candidate $m$, the cell additionally emits
predicted IC$_{50}$ values against five cell lines:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:metallodrug-celllines}
  \mathrm{IC50}(m) \;\in\;
  \mathbb{R}^{5},\qquad
  (\text{A2780},\,\text{MCF7},\,\text{A549},\,
   \text{HCT116},\,\text{OVCAR-3})
\end{equation}
These are not wet-lab measurements; they are the panel of
NCI60-derived predictions consumed by the
$r_\textplatinai$ and $r_\textcytotox$ channels above and
reported as a single tuple per cell. The cell-line panel is
chosen to span three tissue classes (ovarian / breast /
lung + colon) consistent with the NCI60 coverage of
platinum-active and platinum-resistant lines.

\subsubsection{Vertical-specific metrics}
\label{sec:metallodrug-vertical-metrics}

The vertical exposes seven metrics that are \emph{not} part
of the generic typed-term de novo catalogue. The first five
are \textsc{measured} (already shipped); the last two are
\textsc{architectural} (specified here, unit-tested, awaiting
end-to-end \S4 cells).

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{\texttt{metal\_coord\_compliance}} --
        fraction of generated candidates whose metal
        combinator satisfies
        $\Phi_{m}$ of \S\ref{sec:metal-geometry-prior}
        for the metal + oxidation state under
        consideration. Implementation:
        \texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/metrics/metal\_coord\_compliance.py}.
        Already \textsc{measured} in
        \texttt{wf\_lambda\_metal\_pilot/final.md}.

  \item \textbf{\texttt{oxidation\_state\_match}} --
        fraction of generated candidates whose Pt/Pd/Au/Ir/Ru
        centre has oxidation state in the set
        $\{\text{Pt\_II},\,\text{Pt\_IV},\,\text{Au\_I},\,
        \text{Au\_III},\,\text{Pd\_II},\,\text{Ir\_I},\,
        \text{Ir\_III},\,\text{Ru\_II}\}$ \emph{and} is
        consistent with the \texttt{MetalSMILESParser}'s
        charge-state count. Implementation:
        \texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/metrics/oxidation\_state\_match.py}.

  \item \textbf{\texttt{anticancer\_index}} -- composite of
        {\tt coord\_compliance} $\times$ {\tt gsh\_binding}
        $\times$ {\tt dna\_kb}, scaled by
        Lipinski-violation count. Implementation:
        \texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/metrics/anticancer\_index.py}
        (\textsc{measured}, WF-P0-Metrics-Add).

  \item \textbf{\texttt{dna\_kb}} -- DNA-crosslink proxy: the
        number of $\text{Pt-N}_\text{guanine}$ dative bonds
        consistent with a 1,2-intrastrand crosslink
        (the dominant cisplatin adduct). Implementation:
        \texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/metrics/dna\_kb.py}
        (\textsc{measured}, WF-P0-Metrics-Add).

  \item \textbf{\texttt{gsh\_binding}} -- glutathione-binding
        proxy: count of $\text{Pt-S}_\text{thiolate}$ motifs
        consistent with the Pt-GSH detoxification pathway.
        Implementation:
        \texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/metrics/gsh\_binding.py}
        (\textsc{measured}, WF-P0-Metrics-Add).

  \item \textbf{\texttt{aqua\_exchange\_potential}}
        (\textsc{architectural}, NEW) -- counts the number of
        aqua ligands ($\text{Pt-OH}_2$) in the generated
        candidate that can be replaced by an incoming
        nucleophile (azide, amine, thiol). The metric is the
        lead-in for the \texttt{AquaExchange} SMARTS rule of
        \texttt{pt\_click\_compat.py} and is the
        \emph{primary} determinant of the F2(a) MetalLigand-
        Exchange diversity lift. Implementation planned:
        \texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/metrics/aqua\_exchange\_potential.py},
        design at \texttt{TODO/pending/29\_aqua\_exchange\_metric.md}.

  \item \textbf{\texttt{pharma\_relevance\_score}}
        (\textsc{architectural}, NEW) -- weighted combination
        of Lipinski (Rule-of-Five pass $= 1$),
        Veber (rotatable bonds $\leq 10$, TPSA $\leq 140$
        \AA$^2$, both pass $=1$), and ring count $\geq 1$,
        with weights $w_\text{Lipinski} = 0.4$, $w_\text{Veber}
        = 0.4$, $w_\text{ring} = 0.2$. Implementation planned:
        \texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/metrics/pharma\_relevance\_score.py}.
\end{enumerate}

The seven vertical-specific metrics are the per-cell

(\S5), and the failure modes that limit each are catalogued

\subsubsection{Cross-references}
\label{sec:metallodrug-vertical-cross-refs}

The vertical is a specialization of the typed-term de novo
generator of \S\ref{sec:mlc-formalism}--\S\ref{sec:mcts-betanf}
and inherits every architectural commitment of the parent
sub-sections. Concretely:

\begin{itemize}
  \item The five click reactions of
        \S\ref{sec:click-chem-five} (\S3.2) are the
        \emph{only} rule combinators wired into the
        vertical; the typed-reaction signatures of
        \S\ref{sec:click-chem-types} determine which
        Pt-scaffold / Pt-handle pairings are reducible.

  \item The \texttt{MetalGeometryPrior} of
        \S\ref{sec:metal-geometry-prior} (\S3.3) is the
        \emph{hard gate}: candidates violating
        $\Phi_{m}$ are pruned before $\beta$-reduction, so
        the per-cell metrics of
        \S\ref{sec:metallodrug-vertical-metrics} are
        conditional on prior pass.

  \item The MCTS-over-$\beta$-NF driver of
        \S\ref{sec:mcts-betanf} (\S3.4) is the search
        backbone; the per-cell budget
        $n_\text{sim} = 1000$ of
        \S\ref{sec:metallodrug-vertical-protocol} flows
        from the MCTS budget framework.

  \item The Deflex pocket-conditioned wrapper of
        \S\ref{sec:deflex} (\S3.5) is the pocket-side
        wrapper; the 100-pocket CrossDocked2020 split
        enters via the \texttt{PocketMacroSkeleton}
        embedding of \S\ref{sec:deflex-pocket-macro}.

  \item The bond-aware soft prior of
        \S\ref{sec:method:bond-aware-soft-prior} (\S3.7
        anterior) is the secondary-generator bridge that
        will eventually couple the typed-term primary
        generator to the CFM geometric cloud; the
        metallodrug vertical is a primary consumer of this
        bridge once the CFM path-(b) decoder is end-to-end
        retrained. The coupling is \textsc{deferred}
        (\S\ref{sec:method:secondary-cfm},
        \S\ref{sec:future:cfm-deferred}).

  \item The ablation in \S\ref{sec:ablation} (\S5) consumes
        the seven vertical metrics of
        \S\ref{sec:metallodrug-vertical-metrics}; the
        per-cell ablation grid is
        2\,(metal-seed on/off) $\times$ 4\,(seed choice)
        $\times$ 3\,(MCTS seed) $\times$ 5\,(reward-weight
        configurations) $= 120$ cells per pocket, totaling
        $12{,}000$ evaluations on the 100-pocket split.
        This is \textsc{projected} future work
        (\S\ref{sec:future}).

  \item The honest limitations of
        \S\ref{sec:limitations} (\S6) catalog the cell-line
        oracle's training-set bias (\S3.7.4), the
        coordination-table gaps (Fe, Mn, Cu, Rh, Os), and
        the soft-prior / scaffold-aware matrix's Pt-only
        coverage.
\end{itemize}

The five sub-sections \S3.1--\S3.5 together implement the
generic typed-term de novo generator; the present subsection
specializes that generator to the metallodrug vertical by
constraining the atomic vocabulary, restricting the rule
combinators to click chemistry, gating on the metal
refinement predicate, and adding the cell-line-aware oracle
of \S\ref{sec:metallodrug-vertical-oracle}.
