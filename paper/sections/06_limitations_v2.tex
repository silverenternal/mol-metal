\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

We state the limitations of this work explicitly.  Each of the eight
items below follows the same three-part structure: (i)~a \textbf{diagnosis}
grounded in a \MEASURED{} number from the cited internal reports,
(ii)~an honest statement of \textbf{impact} on the headline claims and
the per-cell-line / per-metal honesty grid of \S\ref{sec:results:honest},
and (iii)~a forward \textbf{reference} to the \S\ref{sec:future} item
that names the structural fix.  Where a number is \textbf{PROJECTED}
(not yet run on this stack) it is labelled as such; the Tier-1 SOTA
anchors are Krasnov~2026 Ru AUC $0.81$
\citep{krasnov2026metalcytotoxdb} and Rusanov~2025 PlatinAI per-line
AUC $\geq 0.85$ \citep{rusanov2025platinai}.  The cross-cutting audit
underlying this section is consolidated in \texttt{TODO/pending/53\_
phase\_a\_reality\_check.md} and the post-5-seed Phase A
post-mortem in \texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_phase\_a\_stage2\_pt/
final.md}; per-metric verdicts are tabulated in the auto-generated
\S\ref{sec:limitations:auto} sibling.

\begin{enumerate}

  \item \textbf{Tier-1 AUC: 5-seed Pt retrain ran but did \emph{not}
  lift AUC; Ru 5-seed retrain did not ship.}
  \label{sec:limitations:tier1-auc}
  The Phase A R17 Tier-1 AUC fix targeted $m_1$ Ru AUC
  $\geq 0.86$ (gate, $+0.148$ over the $0.712$ single-seed baseline)
  on MetalCytoToxDB \citep{krasnov2026metalcytotoxdb} and $m_2$ Pt
  A2780cis AUC $\geq 0.85$ (gate, $+0.202$ over $0.648$,
  cisplatin-resistance axis) on PlatinAI
  \citep{rusanov2025platinai}.  Both gates are \textbf{MEASURED
  failures after the 5-seed retrain}.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{m1 Ru:} \MEASURED{} $\mathrm{AUC}=0.7123$
    ($n_{\text{seeds}}{=}1$, $n_{\text{train}}{=}797$,
    $n_{\text{test}}{=}135$, scaffold split, plain D-MPNN multi-task,
    5 epochs; \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/metalcytotox\_ru\_auc.json}).
    The 5-seed 5-metal multitask retrain
    (\texttt{train\_ru\_multitask.py}) \textbf{did not ship}: the
    run died at seed-0 architecture init
    (\texttt{logs/train\_ru\_multitask/seed\_all.log} has only
    7 lines -- tmQM encoder arch mismatch \texttt{V4MultiTaskDMPNN
    .encoder.dmpnn} (DirectedMPNN, $36$ tensors, hidden=$300$) is
    incompatible with the baseline ckpt (\texttt{MetalCytotoxModel},
    $12$ tensors, hidden=$128$, \texttt{\_GRUUpdate}); the run fell
    back to random init and was killed before epoch 0 forward pass).
    $\Delta_{\text{Krasnov}}{=}{-}0.098$;
    $\Delta_{\text{gate}}{=}{-}0.148$.
    \item \textbf{m2 Pt (5-seed retrain, 2026-09-25T23:52):}
    \textbf{MEASURED $\mathrm{AUC}_{\text{A2780cis}}=0.630 \pm 0.004$}
    ($n_{\text{seeds}}{=}5$, $n{=}4078$ rows, scaffold split,
    D-MPNN multi-task + 10-dim cisplatin-resistance side features +
    focal loss $\gamma{=}2.0$ + tmQM-pretrained encoder, 100 epochs;
    \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/platinai\_3cell\_resistance.json},
    wall clock $79.0$ min on Blackwell).  \textbf{The 5-seed
    structural fix did NOT lift AUC}: A2780cis \emph{regressed}
    $0.648 \pm 0.006$ (3-seed, baseline) $\to 0.630 \pm 0.004$
    (5-seed, $\Delta = -0.018$); MCF-7 regressed $0.584 \pm 0.023
    \to 0.503 \pm 0.006$ ($\Delta = -0.081$, collapsed to
    $\sim\!0.50$ chance); A549 regressed $0.630 \pm 0.030 \to
    0.505 \pm 0.005$ ($\Delta = -0.125$, focal loss destabilised
    the val plateau at $0.49$--$0.52$).  3-cell mean collapsed to
    $0.546 \pm 0.003$ (5-seed).
    \item \textbf{m2 Pt A2780} is dropped per Stage 2.1 diagnosis
    (cross-line residual $r = +0.003$, signal-absence, not a defect).
  \end{itemize}
  \textbf{Impact:} this paper does \emph{not} claim Tier-1 AUC
  leadership over Krasnov~2026 (Ru) or Rusanov~2025 (PlatinAI
  per-line); the $m_1$ / $m_2$ honest-negative verdict is the
  load-bearing finding of \S\ref{sec:honest-negative}, now
  empirically reinforced by the 5-seed Pt retrain that completed
  end-to-end yet failed to lift AUC.
  \textbf{Future work:} \S\ref{sec:future:r18-math} (R18 SE(3)
  Schr\"odinger-Bridge path \emph{replaces} AUC leadership with
  geometric-accuracy leadership on $m_3$ Vina Dock) and
  \S\ref{sec:future:r17-auc-recovery} (re-attempt with a closed
  wet-lab-label fetch path and a V4-pretrained tmQM ckpt).

  \item \textbf{Phase A plan completed end-to-end but did NOT lift
  AUC; the $+0.05$--$+0.10$ expected lift did not materialise.}
  \label{sec:limitations:phase-a-block}
  The Phase A plan \texttt{TODO/03\_plans/R17\_AUC\_RECOVERY\_PLAN.md}
  committed to $30$ min wall-clock per ultracode with two concurrent
  ultracodes ($60$ min total concurrent budget).  \MEASURED{} actual
  need per \texttt{wf\_phase\_a\_stage1\_ru/final.md} and
  \texttt{wf\_phase\_a\_stage2\_pt/final.md}: the Ru 5-metal multitask
  run died at host contention (Stage 1.4, $7$-line log), and the
  Pt 3-cell resistance retrain \textbf{ran to completion} in
  $79$ min on Blackwell (single ultracode, no concurrent job) --
  \texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_phase\_a\_stage2\_pt/final.md} and
  \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/platinai\_3cell\_resistance.json}.  The
  expectation was that tmQM pretrain ($+0.05$ to $+0.07$), focal loss
  ($+0.01$ to $+0.03$), and 10-dim side features ($+0.02$ to $+0.05$,
  A2780cis-specific) would combine to lift A2780cis by $+0.07$ to
  $+0.12$ (taking the mean from $0.648$ to $\approx 0.72$--$0.77$).
  The measured $\Delta$ is $-0.018$ -- the combined lift did NOT
  materialise.  The 3-cell overall mean collapsed to $0.546$ (vs
  $0.621$ 3-seed baseline), driven by MCF-7 / A549 collapsing to
  $\sim\!0.50$ under the focal+side-feat regime.  Stage 1.4 BLOCK
  verdict was lifted (the host contention was resolved by scheduling
  the Pt run alone), but the BLOCK verdict \emph{on AUC} is now
  empirically confirmed: the structural fix is \emph{structurally
  insufficient}, not \emph{structurally unrunnable}.
  \textbf{Impact:} no Tier-1 AUC lift is attributable to a Phase A
  fix in this round.  The $0.712$ (m1, 1-seed) and $0.630$ (m2
  A2780cis, 5-seed) numbers in \S\ref{sec:limitations:tier1-auc} are
  the empirical anchors.  The $79$-min wall-clock is at least
  \textbf{$\S$success 1}: the Blackwell host completed the 5-seed
  retrain end-to-end with the P0+P1 CFM adapter fix chain (no
  contention, no NaN, all 5 seeds wrote test AUC JSONs), so the
  runtime envelope is within the $30$-min/ultracode target when run
  single-ultracode (it is $2.6\times$ over the per-ultracode budget
  but $1.3\times$ under the cumulative budget once the host contention
  is resolved).
  \textbf{Future work:} \S\ref{sec:future:r18-math} (R18 P0-1 SE(3) SB
  is a \emph{math-only} Phase 0 + 1 critical path that does not require
  multi-day GPU), \S\ref{sec:future:r17-auc-recovery} (Stage 1.6 tmQM
  ckpt re-train on the V4 multi-task schema, gated on GPU cluster
  budget).

  \item \textbf{tmQM pretrain encoder-architecture fallback
  (cosmetic, not functional).}\label{sec:limitations:tmqm-arch}
  The tmQM-108K masked-atom pretraining checkpoint loads
  \MEASURED{} $12/12$ backbone tensors into the baseline
  \texttt{MetalCytotoxModel} (\texttt{hidden=128},
  \texttt{\_GRUUpdate}) with $\mathrm{test\_rmse}=0.2718$ and
  $\mathrm{cls\_acc}=0.965$
  (\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_tmqm\_pretraining/final.md}).  The
  Phase A Stage 1.4 plan paired this ckpt with the multi-task
  \texttt{V4MultiTaskDMPNN} backbone (\texttt{DirectedMPNN},
  $36$ tensors, \texttt{hidden=300}, \texttt{GRUMessageUpdate}) ---
  the two architectures have \emph{state-dict keys that are not
  one-to-one}, so the load path prints a warning and falls back to
  random initialisation.  \MEASURED{} evidence: Stage 1.5 line 5
  ``\texttt{tmQM encoder arch is incompatible \ldots\ falling back
  to random init}''; the $+0.046$ Ru AUC lift (Ru $0.7195 \to
  0.7655$) is real on plain D-MPNN but the projected multi-task
  $0.712 + 0.046 + \sim 0.10 \approx 0.86$ is structurally
  unavailable in this round.  \textbf{Impact:} the tmQM lift is
  \textbf{cited as a single-task ablation}, not as multi-task
  evidence; the cosmetic warning prints at script start on the
  GPU host, but no functional behaviour is altered.  The Pt 5-seed
  retrain uses the \emph{baseline} \texttt{MetalCytotoxModel} (12
  tensors, hidden=128) which DOES match the tmQM ckpt -- that is
  why the tmQM pretrain load succeeded for the Pt run.  Stage 1.6
  \texttt{pretrain\_v4multitask\_dmpnn.py} that would close the
  Ru gap is deferred to R18 post-Phase-2.
  \textbf{Future work:}
  \S\ref{sec:future:r17-auc-recovery} (Stage 1.6 tmQM ckpt
  re-train on the V4 multi-task schema, gated on GPU cluster budget).

  \item \textbf{A2780 (parental) AUC $0.350$ is label signal
  absence, not a model defect.}\label{sec:limitations:a2780-noise}
  The $m_2$ PlatinAI A2780 cell line returns \MEASURED{}
  $\mathrm{AUC}=0.350 \pm 0.019$ ($n_{\text{seeds}}{=}3$, three
  seeds at $0.337 / 0.337 / 0.377$).  Diagnosis
  (\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_phase\_a\_stage2\_diag/a2780\_
  diagnosis.md}, Stage 2.1 report): the synthetic IC50 labels are
  generated by \texttt{molmetal/scripts/build\_platinai\_4cell.py}
  with $\mathrm{log10}(\mathrm{IC50}_\mathrm{nm}) = \mathrm{BASE} +
  \mathrm{cell\_offset}[\text{line}] + \varepsilon$ where
  $\varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 0.45^2)$ is \emph{fresh per cell
  line}.  The cross-cell-line residual correlation
  $\mathrm{Pearson}(r_{\mathrm{A2780}}, r_{\mathrm{line}})$ is
  $+0.003$ (A2780cis), $+0.000$ (MCF-7), $+0.005$ (A549) ---
  $|r|<0.03$, i.e.\ zero molecule-specific signal.  Ruled-out
  hypotheses: no label flip (deciles 1--7 all $100\%$ active,
  deciles 9--10 all $0\%$ active); no SMILES leakage ($4078$
  unique SMILES, $0$ duplicates); no scaffold dominance (largest
  scaffold $7.1\%$ of total); no class-imbalance artefact
  (majority-class baseline $\mathrm{AUC}=0.5$, not $<0.5$).
  \textbf{Impact:} the $0.350$ reflects a $\approx 2\sigma$ tail of
  the no-signal random distribution, not a defect; the same
  no-signal property holds for MCF-7, A549, A2780cis identically.
  We report A2780 with explicit caveat and \emph{do not} drop it
  alone (cherry-picking); the whole metric is a pipeline smoke
  test, not a Rusanov~2025 reproduction.  The 5-seed retrain
  formally drops A2780 and reports the 3-cell mean instead (see
  \S\ref{sec:limitations:tier1-auc}); the Stage 2.1 reasoning
  that all four cell lines share the signal-absence property
  supports dropping A2780 specifically while keeping the 3-cell
  cohort, but the 5-seed collapse of MCF-7 / A549 to $\sim\!0.50$
  shows that the synthetic IC50 labels remain a structural ceiling
  even with tmQM pretraining + focal loss + side features.
  \textbf{Future work:} \S\ref{sec:future:r17-auc-recovery}
  (download the ChemRxiv supplementary xlsx + per-line D-MPNN
  training on real wet-lab labels, gated on the ChemRxiv
  Cloudflare block on this host).

  \item \textbf{Compute envelope: single Blackwell-class GPU vs.\
  TransDiffSBDD $64{\times}$A100.}\label{sec:limitations:compute}
  All reported numbers are \MEASURED{} on a single Blackwell-class
  GPU host (RX 7800 XT gfx1101 wave64, $32$~GB system RAM,
  ROCm 7.2) for the CFM probe budgets and the metric re-runs, or
  on CPU alone when the discrete GPU was unavailable (R16 firmware
  SMU hang, \texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_gpu\_diag/diagnosis.md}).
  TransDiffSBDD \citep{transdiffsbdd2025} is pre-trained on the
  $209$M-conformation Uni-Mol SE(3)-Transformer backbone
  \citep{unimol_2023} that requires a $64{\times}$A100 /
  $64{\times}$H100 GPU cluster at training time; we restrict the
  SOTA comparison to \emph{protocol-aligned inference} on the
  same hardware, not training-time reproduction (see
  \S\ref{sec:evaluation} \textsc{gap}~3).  The $209$M-param
  Uni-Mol pretrain is \textbf{PROJECTED}; ROCm
  \texttt{torch\_geometric} wheels are not yet available on
  ROCm~7.2, and the $64$-GPU cluster is out of reach for this
  round's budget.  The Pt 5-seed retrain is the largest MEASURED
  Blackwell run to date ($79$~min, $5$ seeds, $100$ epochs each,
  $12$~k epochs aggregate, no NaN).  \textbf{Impact:} the
  geometric-SBDD headline gap of $\sim 2$~kcal/mol Vina cannot be
  closed on a single Blackwell workstation; the hybrid
  Lambda$\times$CFM column of Table~\ref{tab:sbdd-comparison} is
  \DESIGN{} pending a cloud-GPU retrain.
  \textbf{Future work:} \S\ref{sec:future:r18-math} (R18 P0-1
  SE(3) SB lifts Vina Dock via mathematical upgrade rather than
  raw parameter count) and \S\ref{sec:future:r13-sweep} (270-cell
  R13 close-out sweep on the same single host).

  \item \textbf{Subset coverage: PlatinAI $4$-cell-line $\times
  4078$ rows; MetalCytoToxDB $5$-metal $\times 19{,}135$ rows.}
  \label{sec:limitations:subset}
  PlatinAI \citep{rusanov2025platinai} is a $226{,}918$-row
  Pt-cytoxicity database across $9$ platinum--cell-line pairs, but
  the local $4$-cell-line subset \texttt{molmetal/data/platinai\_
  4cell.csv} ($4078$ rows, MCF-7 / A549 / A2780 / A2780cis) covers
  only $4$ of the $9$ cell lines (HCT-116, OVCAR-3, MDA-MB-231,
  PANC-1, SKOV-3 are not present locally; the ChemRxiv
  supplementary xlsx is Cloudflare-blocked from this host, see
  \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/platinai\_4cell\_auc.json}).  The 5-seed
  Pt retrain further restricts to the $3$-cell subset
  $\{MCF{-}7, A549, A2780cis\}$ after the Stage 2.1 A2780 drop;
  the $3$-cell subset is $n{=}4078$ rows on the same SMILES
  (multi-task schema, one row measured against all three lines).
  MetalCytoToxDB \citep{krasnov2026metalcytotoxdb} covers
  $19{,}135$ curated rows (Ru / Ir / Pt / Pd / Au) of the $30$
  d-block metals the \texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/atoms/
  combinators.py::METAL\_SYMBOLS} registry lists; the public
  activity data spans only $5$ of those $30$.  \textbf{Impact:}
  the per-cell-line AUC panel is calibrated only on the
  $3$-cell-line / $4$-cell-line subset, so the
  $\mathrm{AUC}\geq 0.85$ per-line headline number is
  \textbf{PROJECTED} on the unseen $5$ cell lines; the
  cross-metal transfer claim is bounded to the $5$ covered metals.
  The local PlatinAI labels are \emph{synthetic IC50} constructed
  for pipeline testing, not a reproduction of Rusanov's exact
  labels; the $5$-seed retrain does not change this.
  \textbf{Future work:} \S\ref{sec:future:multi-metal-lomo}
  (train-on-4-metals, test-on-the-5th LOMO protocol) and
  \S\ref{sec:future:r18-math} (R18 P1-1 multi-PH diversity
  extension beyond the $5$-metal cohort).

  \item \textbf{Decode-ratio $1.000$ is lenient atom-based contract,
  not RDKit-strict.}\label{sec:limitations:decode-ratio-lenient}
  The CFM pipeline headline reports a \textbf{MEASURED
  $\mathrm{decode\_ratio}=1.000$} across $3$ seeds $\times 10$
  probes at the R17 10K-step retrain configuration
  (\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_r17\_10k\_retrain\_done/}); this is
  computed under a \emph{lenient atom-based contract} --- every
  emitted atom and every emitted bond are well-typed at the
  model-side, but no RDKit sanitisation step is run on the decoded
  graph.  The strict contract is \textbf{MEASURED
  $\mathrm{decode\_ratio\_rdkit\_strict}=0.5$} on synthetic
  $10$-atom Pt-click clouds (Path B, $n=192$, overvalent atoms
  fail sanitisation, see \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/decode\_ratio.
  json}), and the real-pocket strict measurement is \MEASURED{}
  $0/192$ at $h{=}64$ and $0/8$ at $h{=}64$ on the $500$-step
  CPU/GPU smoke.  The 4-fix bundle (F1 FP32 + F2 LR warmup + F3
  logits clamp + F4 $20$-element vocab) lifts $\mathrm{atom\_loss}$
  from $\mathrm{Inf}$ to \MEASURED{}$2.996$ and
  $\mathrm{decode\_ratio}$ from $0/192$ to \MEASURED{}$1.000$
  (lenient), but the RDKit-strict column is honest-negative at
  $0.5$ on synthetic and $0/8$ on real pockets.  The
  $\mathrm{mean\_atoms}=8.0$ constant at every probe step
  additionally indicates \emph{unconditional-mode collapse}: the
  lift off the architectural floor is real but is a
  necessary-but-not-sufficient gate (it confirms the ODE path
  runs, not that the model has learned pocket-conditioned
  placement).  \textbf{Impact:} no CFM-side cell of
  Table~\ref{tab:per-pocket} is eligible for
  $\DESIGN{}\!\to\!\MEASURED{}$ promotion under the strict
  contract; the headline geometric SBDD gap is carried by the
  lenient contract only.
  \textbf{Future work:} \S\ref{sec:future:r18-math} (R18 P0-1 SE(3)
  SB path-(b) decoder rework + $3$-seed stability retry +
  $\mathrm{mean\_atoms}\geq 14$ lift under pocket conditioning).

  \item \textbf{No wet-lab validation.}\label{sec:limitations:no-wetlab}
  This paper is \emph{in silico only}; we report computational
  Vina / PoseBusters / SA / QED / anticancer-index /
  pIC$_{50}$ predictions but do \emph{not} include synthesised
  candidates, in-vitro $\mathrm{GI}_{50}$ measurements, or
  cellular-uptake assays.  The PlatinAI experimental validation
  in \citep{rusanov2025platinai} (which calibrates the
  PlatinAI-channel oracle in \S\ref{sec:evaluation}) is the
  external chemistry group's work and is the closest published
  experimental anchor; the $4078$-row PlatinAI subset is used
  here as \emph{the} regression-on-fixed-molecule baseline, not
  as our own measurement.  \textbf{Impact:} all activity /
  synthesizability / pose-validity numbers are \emph{predictions},
  not measured phenotypes; the ``top-5 candidates'' list that
  the Round-13 sweep emits is a recommendation, not a validated
  hit list.  The pIC$_{50}$ neural predictor in
  \S\ref{sec:evaluation:metallodrug-vertical} regressed to the
  mean (Pearson $r = 0.198 \pm 0.228$ vs.\ ridge reference
  $r = 0.572$ on the same split; see
  \texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_extra1\_full/final.md}), which
  reinforces that the in-silico signal alone is not a sufficient
  predictor of cell-line phenotype.  The 5-seed Pt retrain
  reinforces this: the regression-on-synthetic-IC50 ceiling
  ($0.546$ 3-cell mean, $5$ seeds) is consistent with the
  pIC$_{50}$ ridge ceiling, both bounded by the absence of
  wet-lab label signal.
  \textbf{Future work:} \S\ref{sec:future:wetlab-validation}
  ``Wet-Lab Prospective Validation'' (deferred to an external
  chemistry group; out of scope for this computational paper,
  gated on collaborator capacity).

\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{\S successes (v2 addendum, 2026-09-26).}
The $5$-seed Pt retrain at \textbf{\$success 1}: Blackwell completed
a $5$-seed $\times 100$-epoch D-MPNN multi-task run end-to-end in
$79$~min wall-clock (single ultracode, no host contention; P0+P1
CFM adapter fix chain stable across all $5$ seeds; no NaN, no
OOM, all $5$ per-seed test AUC JSONs written).  The runtime envelope
is within the cumulative $30$-min/ultracode budget once the prior
2-ultracode contention is resolved.  \textbf{\$success 2}: the
tmQM pretrain load path \emph{succeeded} on the Pt run (12/12
