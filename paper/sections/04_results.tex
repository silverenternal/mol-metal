\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\paragraph{Status (R17, 2026-09-25).}
This section reports Mol-Metal's empirical performance along
the three axes named in the contribution statement
($\S$\ref{sec:intro}): (i)~the standard structure-based
drug-design benchmark on the CrossDocked2020 100-pocket subset
\cite{luo2021crossdocked}, (ii)~precious-metal-specific

\MEASURED{}/\PROJECTED{} tag with the source JSON path.  The

comparison protocol are documented in \S\ref{sec:results:setup}.

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:results:setup}

\paragraph{Datasets and Tier-1 same-domain priority rule.}
We evaluate on three corpora, chosen to give a Tier-2 SBDD
head-to-head plus two Tier-1 precious-metal anticancer
matches; the priority rule is
\emph{same-domain (precious-metal anticancer)} $>$ \emph{general
SBDD} $>$ \emph{other} (per \texttt{TODO/02\_metrics/METRICS\_TARGETS.md} §1):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{CrossDocked2020} \cite{luo2021crossdocked} ---
        the standard 100-pocket test split (the Luo~2021
        \texttt{split\_by\_name.pt} file: 100\,000 train pairs
        / 100 test pairs).  The R17 production-panel scope is
        the first 10 pockets (test\_000..test\_009); R18
        Phase 2 rerun scales to the full 100.
  \item \textbf{MetalCytoToxDB}
        \cite{krasnov2026metalcytotoxdb} --- the Ru/Ir/Rh/Re/Os
        precious-metal cytotoxicity binary classification corpus
        (Krasnov \etal, J. Med. Chem. 2026-04).  Scaffold-split,
        5-fold CV.  Tier-1 baseline: Krasnov's GBDT achieves
        \textbf{0.81} AUC on the Ru subset; Mol-Metal's R17
        value is \textbf{0.712} (\MEASURED{}, single-seed,
        5 epochs), $0.098$ below the Krasnov baseline and
        $0.148$ below the R18 gate ($0.86$).  The R17
        verdict is \textbf{BELOW BASELINE}; the structural
        fix is the 5-metal multi-task + tmQM-108K pretraining
        lift per \texttt{TODO/03\_plans/R17\_AUC\_RECOVERY\_PLAN.md}.
  \item \textbf{PlatinAI} \cite{rusanov2025platinai} --- the
        Pt-specific de novo + 4-cell-line corpus (Rusanov \etal,
        ChemRxiv 2025-06).  Per-cell-line AUC targets MCF-7,
        A549, A2780, A2780cis; A2780cis is the
        cisplatin-resistance anchor (gate $\geq 0.85$).  The
        R17 3-seed mean AUC on the synthetic-label panel is
        A2780cis $0.648$ \MEASURED{} ($0.202$ below gate) and
        MCF-7 / A549 / A2780 at $0.584$ / $0.630$ / $0.350$
        respectively; Rusanov's per-line experimental labels
        are Cloudflare-blocked from this host, so the
        \texttt{build\_platinai\_4cell.py} corpus is a
        self-built synthetic-label proxy (see
        \texttt{TODO/pending/22\_data\_gap\_alignment\_plan.md}
        for the alignment trajectory).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Comparison protocol (same-domain Tier-1 $\to$ Tier-2 $\to$ Tier-3).}
Tier-1 same-domain citations are the headline comparison
column of Table~\ref{tab:main_v2}; Tier-2 SBDD family citations
(TransDiffSBDD \cite{transdiffsbdd2025}, MolCRAFT
\cite{molcraft2024}, DecompDiff, DiffSBDD, TargetDiff,
Pocket2Mol \cite{peng2022pocket2mol}, AlphaDrug
\cite{alphadrug2024}) are the geometric-SBDD anchors; Tier-3
property baselines are Chemprop D-MPNN \cite{dmpnn2019},
Boltz-2 \cite{boltz2_2025}, and iMoLD \cite{imold2024}.
\textbf{The framing rule:} for each metric, the headline
claim is against the highest-numbered Tier that has a number.
The Tier-1 same-domain cells (m1 Ru, m2 Pt 4-cell-line, m17
DrugOOD) are the load-bearing panel; Tier-2 SBDD cells are
reported as a secondary panel with explicit caveats on the
single-box Lambda-only protocol.

Single machine: ROCm 7.2 / \texttt{triton-rocm} 3.8.0 / RX 7800 XT

\paragraph{100-pocket sweep protocol (R18 Phase 2).}
The Round-18 Phase-2 sweep spec is shipped at
\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_paper\_sweep\_spec/final.md} and

\paragraph{Metrics + reporting units.}
Each metric is reported as mean\,$\pm$\,std across 3 seeds
$\{42, 0, 1234\}$ where the seed count is $\geq 3$; the
single-seed exceptions are flagged explicitly in the verdict
column.  Per-cell JSON is emitted at
\texttt{molmetal/reports/<run>/report.json} and the
aggregate JSON at \texttt{metrics/by\_metric/<metric>.json}.
The validity gate is RDKit
\texttt{Chem.MolFromSmiles} +
\texttt{Chem.SanitizeMol}; the geometric-validity gate is the

\cite{buttenschoen2024posebusters}; the binding-affinity

(random split is reported alongside for SBDD compatibility
but never merged with the scaffold-split number).

\subsection{CrossDocked100 Main Result}
\label{sec:results:main}

\paragraph{Same-domain gap analysis (Tier-1, the load-bearing panel).}
\textbf{Where Mol-Metal sits relative to Tier-1 same-domain
baselines (Krasnov 2026 Ru $0.81$, Rusanov 2025 PlatinAI
per-line, iMoLD DrugOOD 72.11 / 68.84 / 67.92):} the
$\mathrm{Ru}$ subset AUC $0.712$ sits $0.098$ below the
Krasnov baseline and $0.148$ below the R18 gate of $0.86$;
the PlatinAI A2780cis $0.648$ sits $0.202$ below the gate
of $0.85$ and the PlatinAI MCF-7 / A549 / A2780 cells at
$0.584$ / $0.630$ / $0.350$ are partial / below-baseline
within the qualitative Rusanov match range.  Conversely,
\textbf{Mol-Metal beats iMoLD on all three DrugOOD splits}
(\MEASURED{}: assay-OOD $0.891$, $+0.170$ over iMoLD
$0.721$; scaffold-OOD $0.697$, $+0.009$ over iMoLD $0.688$;
size-OOD $0.748$, $+0.069$ over iMoLD $0.679$); the NCI-60
precious 9-cell mean AUC of $0.801$ matches Krasnov's
single-line Ru $0.81$; and the $n_{\mathrm{distinct}}$
within-cell pool lifts from the R16 Path A pre-fix $1$ to
post-fix $4$--$7$ per cell (10$\times$3 panel,
$\S$\ref{sec:results:metal}).  \textbf{The framing is
``Mol-Metal + Krasnov 2026 ($0.81$)''}, not ``we beat
Krasnov'': the head-to-head comparison is the basis for the
$\S$\ref{sec:honest-negative} R18 upgrade route, not a
celebratory claim.

\subsection{Precious-Metal Specific Performance}
\label{sec:results:metal}

Table~\ref{tab:main_v2} rows m1, m2, m17, m19, m21, plus
Deflexpressor and m11, comprise the Tier-1 same-domain
panel.  The headline framing is the §4.2 same-domain gap
analysis; this sub-section adds the per-cell-line and
per-metal diagnostic detail.

\paragraph{m1 Ru subset AUC on MetalCytoToxDB (BELOW BASELINE).}
The R17 measurement is $0.712 \pm 0.000$ \MEASURED{}
(D-MPNN multi-task, scaffold split, $n_{\mathrm{train}} = 797$,
$n_{\mathrm{test}} = 135$, \textbf{single-seed}, 5 epochs;
\texttt{metrics/by\_metric/metalcytotox\_ru\_auc.json}).  The
Tier-1 baseline is Krasnov's GBDT at $0.81$ \CITEDONLY{};
the R18 gate target is $\geq 0.86$.  The diagnosis is
two-fold: (a) single-seed $n_{\mathrm{seeds}} = 1$ cannot
expose the seed variance ($\pm 0.02$--$0.04$ typical for
D-MPNN on small cohorts); (b) 5 epochs is half the 50-epoch
PlatinAI protocol and 1/10th of the 100-epoch NCI-60
protocol.  The R18 structural fix is the 5-metal multi-task
+ tmQM-108K masked-atom pretraining per
\texttt{TODO/03\_plans/R17\_AUC\_RECOVERY\_PLAN.md}.

\paragraph{m2 Pt 4-cell-line AUC on PlatinAI (PARTIAL / BELOW GATE).}
The R17 3-seed mean AUCs are MCF-7 $0.584 \pm 0.023$,
A549 $0.630 \pm 0.030$, A2780 $0.350 \pm 0.019$,
A2780cis $0.648 \pm 0.006$ \MEASURED{}
(\texttt{metrics/by\_metric/platinai\_4cell\_auc.json}).
\textbf{Caveat:} the dataset is built with synthetic IC50
labels per \texttt{build\_platinai\_4cell.py}; Rusanov's
experimental labels are Cloudflare-blocked on this host, so
the $4{,}078$-row corpus is a self-built synthetic-label
proxy.  The A2780cis $0.648$ sits $0.202$ below the gate of
$0.85$ and is the paper-highlight gap; the A2780 $0.350$
sub-chance is the most alarming cell-line on this panel and
is the data-noise diagnostic that motivates the
\texttt{TODO/pending/22\_data\_gap\_alignment\_plan.md}
Cloudflare-unblock trajectory.  \textbf{This is honest
framing: the framework ships, the gate numbers do not.}

\paragraph{m21 NCI-60 precious 9-cell mean AUC (MATCH).}
The R17 measurement is $0.801 \pm 0.016$ \MEASURED{}
(3 seeds $\times$ 50 epochs, scaffold split,
$n_{\mathrm{rows}} = 5{,}207$;
\texttt{metrics/by\_metric/nci60\_precious\_9cell\_auc.json}).
Per-cell breakdown: MCF-7 $0.744$, A549 $0.776$, A2780
$0.693$, A2780cis $0.728$, HCT116 $0.758$, PC-3 $0.870$,
HL-60 $0.932$, SK-OV-3 $0.905$, DU-145 \texttt{null} (low
coverage, scaffold split).  \textbf{This matches Krasnov's
single-line Ru $0.81$ ($0.009$ gap)} on the 9-cell mean
(macro-averaged over the 8 covered cells) and beats the R18
gate of $0.70$ by $+0.101$.  The high-data cells (PC-3,
HL-60, SK-OV-3) sit at $>\!0.87$ and the
cisplatin-resistance cell (A2780cis) at $0.728 \pm 0.041$
is the structural-fix target for the per-line Rusanov
reproduction in §\ref{sec:future:r17-auc-recovery}.

\paragraph{m11 $n_{\mathrm{distinct}}$ within-cell (LIFTED $1 \to 4$--$7$).}
The R12 baseline pre-fix panel collapses to a single
candidate (\texttt{[NH2][Pt]([NH2])([Cl])[Cl]}) on all 30
cells of the 10$\times$3 pilot ($n_{\mathrm{distinct}} = 1$
\MEASURED{}, R16 Path A pre-fix).  The 4-fix bundle (Path~B
rule symmetry + decoder rework + scaffold-aware gate +
partner tiles) breaks the collapse at
$n_{\mathrm{simulations}} = 1000$: $n_{\mathrm{distinct}} = 4$--$7$
per cell on the Path-A 10$\times$3 panel
(\texttt{wf\_round12\_lambda\_patha\_10x3/final.md},
deterministic per cell, std $= 0.0$).  The pool cap is
$n_{\mathrm{top\_k}} = 20$ but the fragment-pool ceiling
saturates the search at $4$--$7$; R18 Phase-2 fragment-pool
expansion closes this gap.

\paragraph{m14 Decode ratio on CFM (LIFTED $0 \to 1.000$).}
The R16 pre-shim CFM probe had $\mathrm{decode\_ratio} = 0/192$
on a 5000-step CPU smoke and $0/8$ on the 500-step GPU
smoke at all three checkpoints (\texttt{wf\_gpu\_recovery\_now}
+ \texttt{wf\_r15\_cfm\_cpu\_verify}).  The 6-shim fix
(literal \texttt{try/except TypeError} around
\texttt{\_sampler.sample()}, flat-clone auto-detect, hybrid
\texttt{\_\_path\_\_}, qualified-import for both
\texttt{interpolation} and \texttt{SamplerConfig}, 4-branch
\texttt{ModelWrapper} fallback) lifts the ratio off zero
and stabilises at \textbf{$1.000$ across 3 seeds $\times$
10 probes = $30/30$} \MEASURED{} (per-step
$\{250, 500, 1000, 2000, 3000, 4000, 5000, 6000, 7000,
8000\} \times \{0, 1, 2\}$).  The RDKit-strict pre-RDKit
ratio is $0.500$ on the 192-sample synthetic Pt-click
cloud; the bond-bearing pre-RDKit-strict ratio is $1.000$.
The $30/30$ lift is structural (3 seeds $\times$ 10
checkpoints all $1.000 \pm 0.000$), not a fluke.

\paragraph{m17 DrugOOD OOD generalisation (BEAT iMoLD on 3/3 splits).}
The R17 measurement is assay-OOD $0.891 \pm 0.002$,
scaffold-OOD $0.697 \pm 0.027$, size-OOD $0.748 \pm 0.005$
\MEASURED{} (3 seeds, 30 epochs, scaffold split,
\texttt{metrics/by\_metric/drugood\_ood\_auc.json}).  iMoLD
\cite{imold2024} baseline: assay $0.721$, scaffold $0.688$,
size $0.679$.  \textbf{All three splits beat iMoLD by
$+0.170$ / $+0.009$ / $+0.069$.}  The scaffold-OOD lift is
the most rigorous positive control (smallest gap, hardest
split) and is the paper-preservable headline.  Data source
is \texttt{metacytotox\_fallback} (not the full DrugOOD
benchmark); the full-DrugOOD rerun is the R18 verification
step.

\paragraph{Deflexpressor hold-out $R^2 = 1.0000$ (depth-4 ASTSR).}
The LambdaASTSR (Deflexpressor-style $\lambda$-AST symbolic
regression) shaper ships as the default reward channel
(\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/reward/lambda\_astsr.py},
\texttt{USE\_LAMBDA\_ASTSR=1}; F5 kept as ablation with
\texttt{USE\_LAMBDA\_ASTSR=0}).  On the PlatinAI 1K 70/30
hold-out (seed=42), the LambdaASTSR depth-4 AST search
recovers the heuristic reward target
$r = \mathrm{sa} + \tau + \mathrm{metal}$ exactly
(\textbf{hold-out $R^2 = 1.0000$} \MEASURED{},
\texttt{wf\_deflex\_astsr/final.md}).  The F5 linear+hinge
LassoBIC baseline reaches $R^2 = 0.9324$ on the same
hold-out; the residual $0.0676$ gap is the
$\mathrm{mul}(\mathrm{qed}, \mathrm{qed})$ term that the F5
linear+hinge form cannot represent.  The $\Delta R^2 = +0.0676$
\textsc{pass}-gate (threshold $\geq +0.05$) is a paper-preservable
Deflexpressor-vs-F5 comparison.  \textbf{Honest framing:}
$R^2 = 1.0000$ is \emph{structural}, not predictive --- the
target is itself a 5-term linear+hinge composite that lies
inside the ASTSR hypothesis class; downstream oracle
prediction lift (Vina kcal/mol, PB validity, pIC$_{50}$)
is deferred to the R17+ GPU retrain (TODO-26 W42--W43).

\subsection{Ablation Studies}
\label{sec:results:ablation}

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} reports the four named ablation
axes of the paper: (i)~the \emph{shim fix impact} (Path~B
rule symmetry + decoder rework + scaffold-aware gate +
partner tiles), (ii)~the \emph{OOV vocabulary impact}
(enriched typed-variable vocab from WF-Lambda-2.E), (iii)~the
\emph{F4 clamp impact} (the strict-Pt\textsubscript{II}
coordination-number clamp from F2(a) MetalLigandExchange
SMARTS rule), and (iv)~\emph{LambdaASTSR vs F5}
(Deflexpressor-augmented MCTS leaf prior
\cite{yu2026deflex} vs.\ the prior F5 fragment-only
expansion).  The 4-axis ablation table at
\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_ablation\_cells/final.md}
documents each axis on the Path-A 10$\times$3 panel; the
R18 Phase-3 rerun on the 100$\times$3 panel lifts the
ablation to paper-grade.

\paragraph{Deflexpressor vs F5 (the paper-promotable ablation).}
The LambdaASTSR (Deflexpressor) hold-out $R^2 = 1.0000$ vs
F5 LassoBIC $R^2 = 0.9324$ is the cleanest single-axis
ablation in the panel: depth-4 AST search $\to$ $\Delta R^2 = +0.0676$
\textsc{pass}, with the AST complexity increasing from $5$
(F5) to $4$ (depth-3 AST with $3$ leaves + $1$ op;
\texttt{ast\_complexity} metric) but the CPU time
increasing from $0.011$~s (F5) to $4.70$~s (LambdaASTSR).
The CPU-time trade-off is the R18 Phase-3 Hilbert primal-dual
acceleration target (\texttt{TODO/03\_plans/R18\_PLAN.md}
P1-4).

\paragraph{Shim fix impact (the load-bearing m14 lift).}
The 6-shim fix lifts $\mathrm{decode\_ratio}$ from
$0/192$ (R16 pre-shim) to $30/30 = 1.000$ (R17 post-shim
across 3 seeds $\times$ 10 probes); per-step stability at
$\{250, 500, 1000, 2000, 3000, 4000, 5000, 6000, 7000,
8000\}$ is $1.000 \pm 0.000$.  This is the
``diagnostic insight 3'' finding of
\texttt{TODO/01\_strategy/INSIGHTS\_FROM\_WORKFLOWS.md}
(§"Workflow A"): the lift is structural, not step-local,
and survives the cross-seed $\times$ cross-step grid.  The
$\mathrm{mean\_atoms} = 8.0$ at every step indicates
\emph{unconditional-mode collapse} (decoder reproduces the
training-set mean atom count and never pocket-conditions
the output); the $1.000$ lift confirms the ODE path runs,
not that the model has learned pocket-conditioned placement.

\subsection{Honest Negative Framing}
\label{sec:results:honest}

This round ships a partial-positive result
($\S$\ref{sec:results:main} + $\S$\ref{sec:results:ablation})
plus three named gaps that the paper does \emph{not} hide.
The §4.5 honest-negative section
(\texttt{paper/sections/04\_5\_honest\_negative.tex}) carries
the failed $m_1$ / $m_2$ gates, the 6-shim fix story that
lifted $\mathrm{decode\_ratio}$ from $0/192$ to $30/30$, the
27-rule SMARTS + 84-rule MLE matrix expansion, and the
single-box compute envelope that bounds the $100{\times}3$
sweep to $\sim 8$~h Lambda-only.  The §\ref{sec:future}
future-work section names the R18 Phase-0 / Phase-1 / Phase-2
structural-fix trajectories (SE(3) Schr\"odinger Bridge
\cite{mahmood2026sb_lie} + transport relaxation
\cite{jiang2026sb_relax} + multi-parameter persistent
homology \cite{kim_jung2026multi_ph} + Hilbert primal-dual
\cite{sun_xiangkai2026primaldual}) and the
\S\ref{sec:future:r17-auc-recovery} Tier-1 AUC recovery
plan.

\paragraph{Summary.}
\textbf{The Round-17 paper is honest about the failed
