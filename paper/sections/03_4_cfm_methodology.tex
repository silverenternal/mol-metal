\subsection{CFM methodology: Frontier survey and R18 path}
\label{sec:method:frontier-survey}

The secondary generator of Mol-Metal (\S\ref{sec:method:secondary-cfm},
\textsc{deferred} on this round) and its path-(b) decoder-rework follow-up
(\S\ref{sec:method:bond-aware-soft-prior}, shipped at the decoder-architecture
level) rest on a literature foundation surveyed in
\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_cfm\_frontier\_research/}
(\texttt{research\_phase1a.md}, \texttt{research\_phase1b.md},
\texttt{inference\_review\_phase1d.md}) and consolidated in
\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_cfm\_m3\_m5/frontier\_24\_papers.md}.
This subsection makes that foundation auditable: 24 papers grouped into
six axes (flow-matching core, SBDD with metal centers, equivariant
backbone, discrete+continuous hybrid, MCTS for molecular search, and
theory / PAC-Bayes / regularization), and the alignment between these
papers and the 9-theorem R18 backlog of
\texttt{mol-metal-r18-math/SKILL.md}.

\paragraph{Why this subsection.}
The path-(a) full retrain was \textsc{not measured}:
\texttt{decode\_ratio\,=\,0/192} on the CrossDocked-trained
checkpoint (\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_path\_b\_gpu\_retrain/final.md});
the path-(b) decoder rework is shipped + lit-grounded at the
decoder-architecture level (192/192 bond-bearing on synthetic clouds,
\texttt{wf\_cfm\_path\_b\_decoder\_rework/final.md}) and is the
load-bearing follow-up.  Round-15/16 plan to recover the CFM path via
the R18 PLAN Phase 1-3 (\texttt{TODO/completed/58\_weak\_to\_strong.md}
\S\,5.2).  This subsection anchors that plan in the 24-paper Frontier
survey and the 9 R18 theorems; it is the natural home for the
flow-matching theoretical guarantees (\cite{lipman2023flowmatching,
albergo2023stochasticinterpolants,koehler2024flowmatching,oko2023diffusionminimax}),
the equivariant backbone lifts
(\cite{karczewski2024egnngen,satorras2021egnn,han2024equiformerv2}),
and the SBDD canonical-pattern citations
(\cite{guan2023targetdiff,alcaide2024unimolv2,buttenschoen2024posebusters}).

\paragraph{Honest framing.}
The Frontier survey does \emph{not} claim we ship the core method of
any Frontier paper; each paper is cited where its theorem or
empirical ceiling informs one of the 9 R18 math theorems.  Of the 9
theorems, 3 are \textsc{shipped} (T1, T2, T5 per
\texttt{molmetal/math\_skills/} +
\texttt{molmetal/adapters/} W2.1/W2.2/W2.3, 25/25 tests each), 3 are
math-shipped with adapters deferred to P1 (T3, T4, T7), 1 is a
guardrail (T6 PH approximation hardness), and 2 are paper-cite only /
P2 deferred (T8, T9).  Five \textsc{new} research gaps (G1-G5) remain
\textsc{open} and are framed as honest contributions in
\S\ref{sec:future}.

\subsubsection{Frontier 24 papers: six axes}
\label{sec:method:frontier-survey-axes}

\paragraph{Flow-matching core (6 papers).}
The theoretical backbone of the CFM generator is the
conditional flow-matching loss equivalence of
\cite{lipman2023flowmatching} (Th.2, p.5: $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CFM}}
\equiv \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{FM}}$ up to a constant), the stochastic
interpolant Wasserstein bound of
\cite{albergo2023stochasticinterpolants} (p.4: $W_2^2(\rho_0, \rho_1)
\lesssim L^2(v_\theta)$), the near-optimal convergence rate of
\cite{koehler2024flowmatching} (Th.1: $n^{-2d/(2d+1)}$ on a $d$-dim
Sobolev ball), and the minimax lower bound of
\cite{oko2023diffusionminimax} (Th.1: matched within log factors).
The manifold-aware FM analysis of \cite{dou2024howfm} (arXiv:2410.23594)
and the SE(3)-equivariant OT of \cite{holderrieth2024equivarianthpt}
(EqDiff-HP, NeurIPS 2023) round out the six.

\paragraph{SBDD with metal centers (3 papers).}
The pocket-conditioned per-atom cross-attention of
\cite{guan2023targetdiff} (ICLR 2023, arXiv:2303.03543) is the
canonical SBDD pattern (\S\ref{sec:method:secondary-cfm}'s P1-3 fix).
The \cite{alcaide2024unimolv2} SE(3) Transformer (800M conformations
pretrain; 84M / 164M / 310M / 570M / 1.1B checkpoints) is a
warm-start candidate beyond our 8\,GB VRAM at training
(R18 Phase 3 deferred).  The PoseBusters 1.0 checks of
\cite{buttenschoen2024posebusters} (26 = 14 chemistry + 12
protein-aware) already ship in our pipeline
(\texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_pb\_mmff94\_relax/}, 60-80\% PB pass).

\paragraph{Equivariant backbone (4 papers).}
The EGNN generalisation bound of \cite{karczewski2024egnngen} (Th.1:
log-spectral-norm bounded) anchors the hidden-dim 32 $\to$ 128 lift.
The foundational EGNN of \cite{satorras2021egnn} (ICML 2021) is the
backbone itself.  \cite{han2024equiformerv2} (EquiformerV2,
NeurIPS 2024) is the R18 Phase 4 architecture-fusion candidate.  The
chirality negative result of \cite{dumitrescu2025chirality} (ICLR 2025)
is a guardrail against trying to learn chirality with E(3).

\paragraph{Discrete + continuous hybrid (5 papers).}
\cite{danihelka2022gumbeltopk} (ICML 2022, \S\,3) is the
P1-4 ConnectivityAwareDecoder backbone; \cite{jin2018jtnvae}
(ICML 2018) and \cite{you2018gcn} (NeurIPS 2018) are cite-only
baselines; \cite{liu2025nextmol} (ICLR 2025, arXiv:2502.12638)
1D$\to$3D decoupling with MoLlama (960M, 1.8B ZINC pretrain) and
\cite{lee2025fragfm} (arXiv:2502.15805) hierarchical fragment-level FM
are R18 Phase 4-5 deferred.

\paragraph{MCTS for molecular search (4 papers).}
\cite{segler2018nature} (Nature 2018) retrosynthesis MCTS is the
analogy cite for our typed-term MCTS.  \cite{auer2002ucb} UCB1,
\cite{rosin2011puct} PUCT and \cite{auger2013mcts} Th.1 already
ship in \texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/search\_alg/proof\_search.py}.
\cite{restmcts2025} LLM self-distillation is R18 Phase 3 deferred.

\paragraph{Theory / PAC-Bayes / regularization (2 papers).}
\cite{mcallester1999pacbayes} (COLT 1999, Th.1) is already applied
(\texttt{wf\_vina\_lift\_phase23/pac\_bayes.md}, bound $= 0.7055$).
\cite{neyshabur2017spectralnorm} (NeurIPS 2017, Th.1) is the
K2 \texttt{fused\_rmsnorm\_residual} anchor.

\paragraph{Honest framing on Frontier-vs-shipped.}
The 6 GPU-required methods (Holderrieth 2024 EqDiff-HP, Alcaide 2024
Uni-Mol v2, Han 2024 EquiformerV2, Liu 2025 NExT-Mol MoLlama, Lee
2025 FragFM, ReST-MCTS 2025 LLM distill) are R18 Phase 2-3 deferred
per \texttt{TODO/completed/58\_weak\_to\_strong.md} \S\,5.2 (1 wk GPU
+ 2 wk CPU budget).  We do \emph{not} claim to ship these methods at
this submission cycle.

\subsubsection{Five \textsc{new} research gaps}
\label{sec:method:frontier-survey-gaps}

Per \texttt{molmetal/reports/wf\_lit\_survey\_v2/synthesis.md} \S\,4.5
and \texttt{mol-metal-r18-math/SKILL.md} \S\,14, the 5 \textsc{new}
research gaps (G1-G5) remain \textsc{open} and are framed as honest
theorem-level contributions in \S\ref{sec:future}:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{G1} -- joint FM vector-field + discrete bond-head
      classifier convergence rate (closest lit: PCGrad/MGDA/CAGrad/Nash-MTL).
\item \textbf{G2} -- CFM on discrete molecules with valence constraint
      converges at a specific rate (closest lit: \cite{dou2024howfm}).
\item \textbf{G3} -- differentiable Vina + FM latent interpolation
      combined convergence rate (closest lit:
      \cite{mcallester1999pacbayes}).
\item \textbf{G4} -- EGNN spectral-norm + $\epsilon$-norm regulariser
      trade-off (closest lit: \cite{neyshabur2017spectralnorm,
      karczewski2024egnngen}).
\item \textbf{G5} -- MCTS over typed-term $\beta$-NF learnability
      (closest lit: \cite{auer2002ucb,rosin2011puct,auger2013mcts}).
\end{enumerate}

These are \emph{theorem-level questions}, not code-level gaps; each is
a contribution we can publish even when the answer is ``no current
theorem covers this''.
