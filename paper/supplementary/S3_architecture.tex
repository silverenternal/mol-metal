\subsection*{S3.1 Backbone chain: EGNN -> YuelBond -> W-Flow -> MacroGuide -> CoordGNN -> QuotientDiff}
\label{supp:s3-backbone}

The backbone chain runs left-to-right during training; at
inference time only W-Flow + QuotientDiff are exercised end-to-end
(the rest are conditioning features consumed as side inputs).

\paragraph{EGNN (Satorras et al.\ 2021).}
5-layer EGNN at \texttt{molmetal/adapters/egnn\_predictor.py};
node features $h_{i}^{(0)} = (z_{i}, h_{\mathrm{atom}}) \in
\mathbb{R}^{32}$; edge features $e_{ij}^{(0)} = (z_{i}, z_{j},
h_{\mathrm{bond}}) \in \mathbb{R}^{16}$; equivariant messages
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:egnn-message}
  m_{ij} \;=\; \phi_{e}\!\bigl( h_{i}, h_{j}, \|x_{i} - x_{j}\|^{2},
                            e_{ij} \bigr), \quad
  x_{i}^{\prime} \;=\; x_{i} + \sum_{j \neq i} (x_{i} - x_{j})\,
                          \phi_{x}(m_{ij}).
\end{equation}

\paragraph{YuelBond decoder (R18 P2-3).}
\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/lam\_chem/yuelbond\_decoder.py} is
a 3-stage 2-layer MLP per stage with skip connections that takes
the EGNN edge features and predicts the bond-order tensor over
$\{\text{covalent, dative, aromatic, hydrogen}\}$.  $\approx 0.41$
M params.

\paragraph{W-Flow (Lipman 2023).}
\texttt{molmetal/adapters/flow\_matching\_lipman/} wraps an 8-layer
EGNN velocity net that predicts $\mathrm{d} X_{t} / \mathrm{d} t$
given pocket embedding + atom features + bond head output.
Interpolant is Euclidean $X_{t} = (1 - t) X_{0} + t X_{1}$; R18
W2.1 replaces it with the SE(3) geodesic of M22.

\paragraph{MacroGuide (Maksymiuk 2026) + CoordGNN + QuotientDiff.}
MacroGuide PH at \texttt{macroguide\_ph.py} is the R18 P2-2
CPU-only skeleton; ships the ring-size histogram +
aromaticity-aware persistence proxy as $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$
($n_{\mathrm{bins}} = 8$ default), no learned parameters; W2.x
will integrate the full differentiable Vietoris--Rips.  CoordGNN
is a 4-layer \texttt{coordination\_gnn.py} that consumes the EGNN
pairwise features and predicts per-atom coordination numbers over
$\{0, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$; $\approx 0.27$ M params, conditioning
input to the MetalGeometryPrior gate.  QuotientDiff at
\texttt{quotient\_space\_diffusion.py} replaces the Euclidean
$(B, N, 3)$ output of W-Flow with a rotation-translation-
equivariant output on the $\mathrm{SE}(3) / (\mathrm{SO}(3)
\ltimes \{0\}) = \mathbb{R}^{3}$ quotient; 6-layer EGNN denoise
net, $\approx 0.56$ M params.

\paragraph{Integration surface.}
The backbone chain is consumed by (i) the RewardAggregator
(7-channel reward head); (ii) the \texttt{BondAwareDecoder} of
the CFM pocket-conditioned pipeline; and (iii) the
\texttt{MultiPHPocketFeatures} adapter of W2.3.  No module in the
chain is mutated by the R18 upgrade path; each R18 adapter wraps
the chain with an \texttt{nn.Module} that exposes the chain's
outputs as side inputs.

\subsection*{S3.2 Lambda x CFM coupling adapter}
\label{supp:s3-coupling}

\paragraph{Architecture.}
The Lambda x CFM coupling adapter at
\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/lam\_chem/coupling\_adapter.py}
ships as a torch-free numpy 2-layer MLP that maps a 9-d pocket
feature vector to a 64-d embedding (the Pocket2Mol convention).
Opt-in via \texttt{COUPLING\_ENABLED} env var (default off); the
\texttt{augment\_pocket\_embedding} wrapper in W2.3 is the
matching opt-in for the 2-PH feature map.

\paragraph{Training script.}
Numpy MLP pre-trained by
\texttt{molmetal/scripts/tmqm\_cfm\_pretraining.py} on the tmQM
corpus (or, in --dry-run mode, on an 8-mol subset).  Checkpoint is
.npz with keys \texttt{W1, b1, W2, b2, gamma, beta}.
\texttt{load\_coupling\_adapter} returns a deterministic stub
adapter when the checkpoint is missing.

\paragraph{Honest framing.}
64-d embedding is a real pocket-feature function, but the MLP was
trained on 8 mols in --dry-run mode (or, when tmQM is mounted, on
the first 8 rows).  It is a stand-in for the real CFM pocket
embedding.  Bit-for-bit determinism and the shape contract are
preserved.

\subsection*{S3.3 Variance MCTS node structure}
\label{supp:s3-variance-mcts}

\paragraph{Node structure.}
The Variance MCTS node at
\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/search\_alg/
variance\_mcts\_integration.py:VarianceMCTSNode} is a
self-contained variance-aware MCTS node; given prior
$P(\cdot \mid s)$ and per-arm statistics
$\{(Q(s, a), N(s, a), \sigma(s, a))\}$, the node implements the
variance-penalised PUCT rule of Wf-11 \S 3 in one class.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \small
  \caption{VarianceMCTSNode field layout.  All fields are per-arm
  attributes; the node carries one copy per arm in $\mathcal{A}(s)$.}
  \label{tab:variance-mcts-fields}
  \begin{tabular}{lll}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Field} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Description} \\
    \midrule
    \texttt{q\_sa} & \texttt{float} & running mean of reward \\
    \texttt{n\_sa} & \texttt{int} & visit count of $a$ at $s$ \\
    \texttt{sigma\_sa} & \texttt{float} & empirical std of reward \\
    \texttt{n\_s} & \texttt{int} & visit count of $s$ \\
    \texttt{p\_sa} & \texttt{float} & prior $P(a \mid s)$ \\
    \texttt{c\_puct} & \texttt{float} & exploration constant ($1.5$) \\
    \texttt{c\_var} & \texttt{float} & variance penalty ($0.5$) \\
    \texttt{n\_min\_visits} & \texttt{int} & threshold ($5$) \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Score function.}
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:variance-puct-score}
  \mathrm{score}(a) \;=\; Q(s, a)
        \;+\; c_{\mathrm{puct}}\, P(a \mid s)\,
                  \frac{ \sqrt{N(s)} }{ 1 + N(s, a) }
        \;-\; c_{\mathrm{var}}\, \frac{ \sigma(s, a) }
                                       { 1 + N(s, a) }
\end{equation}
when $N(s, a) \ge n_{\mathrm{min}}^{\mathrm{visits}}$, otherwise
the variance term is disabled.  When $\sigma(s, a) = 0$ the
variance term vanishes exactly and the canonical PUCT rule of
Silver 2016 + Rosin 2011 is recovered.

\paragraph{Wrapper class.}
\texttt{VarianceMCTSWrapper} wraps an existing
\texttt{MCTSProofSearch} instance and exposes
\texttt{search(\dots)} / \texttt{tree\_statistics} so callers can
attach variance control to the R17 MCTS without patching its
internals (read-mostly; calls \texttt{MCTSProofSearch.search} to
get the candidate list, never mutates the search state machine).

\paragraph{Integration with Score-Repellent MC.}
The variance-MCTS node is the inner-loop selector consumed by the
SynFlowNet advantage estimator at the leaf-state level: MCTS walk
gated by Eq.~\eqref{eq:variance-puct-score}, leaf sampled by the
Score-Repellent MC walk of \S\ref{supp:s2-m26}, seven-channel
\texttt{RewardAggregator} scores the leaf, SynFlowNet advantage
estimator receives the variance-controlled reward.

\subsection*{S3.4 Hilbert-ASTSR composite}
\label{supp:s3-hilbert-astsr}

\paragraph{Architecture.}
The Hilbert-ASTSR composite at
\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/reward/
hilbert\_astsr\_integration.py} combines the Hilbert-space
primal-dual framework of \S\ref{supp:s1-hilbert} with the ASTSR
multiparameter persistence reward of
\texttt{molmetal/molmetal\_lam/reward/
astsr\_multiph\_hilbert.py}.  The composite ships as a single
\texttt{nn.Module} that takes the MCTS leaf state, runs the
Hilbert-space primal-dual iteration on the ASTSR feature map, and
returns the saddle-point value as the reward channel.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \small
  \caption{Hilbert-ASTSR composite layer-wise shapes.  ASTSR
  feature map is $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$; Hilbert-space primal-
  dual layer operates on
  $\mathcal{H}_{1} = \mathcal{H}_{2} = \mathbb{R}^{n_{\mathrm{bins}}}$.}
  \label{tab:hilbert-astsr-shapes}
  \begin{tabular}{lll}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Layer} & \textbf{In} & \textbf{Out} \\
    \midrule
    ASTSR feat.   & $(B, n_{\mathrm{astsr}})$ & $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$ \\
    Linear ($K$)  & $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$ & $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$ \\
    OTPD iter.    & $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$ & $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$ \\
    Ergodic avg.  & $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$ & $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$ \\
    Reward head   & $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$ & $(B, 1)$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Component wiring.}
The composite is a 3-stage module:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{ASTR feature extraction} (line $\sim$120 of
        \texttt{astsr\_multiph\_hilbert.py}).  Takes the MCTS leaf
        state, extracts the aromaticity-aware ring-size histogram,
        then concatenates with the multi-PH feature map of W2.3 to
        produce the $(B, n_{\mathrm{bins}})$ ASTSR feature vector.
  \item \textbf{Hilbert-space primal-dual iteration} (line
        $\sim$240 of \texttt{hilbert\_astsr\_integration.py}).
        Implements the OTPD iteration of M25 with $K$ the
        $n_{\mathrm{bins}} \times n_{\mathrm{bins}}$ identity and
        $G$, $F^{\ast}$ the SW-kernel cost and positivity-cone
        indicator.
  \item \textbf{Reward head} (line $\sim$340 of
        \texttt{hilbert\_astsr\_integration.py}).  2-layer MLP
        that maps the ergodic average to a scalar reward in
        $[0, 1]$; appended to the seven-channel
        \texttt{RewardAggregator} as the $r_{\mathrm{astsr}}$
        channel.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Honest framing.}
Composite is \DESIGN{} (CPU-only \texttt{nn.Module}).  Seven-
channel reward is the live \texttt{RewardAggregator} of R17;
$r_{\mathrm{astsr}}$ is opt-in via \texttt{--enable-astsr}; the
projected reward contribution is \PROJECTED{}.

% ===========================================================================
\paragraph{Summary.}
Four component groups: backbone chain (six modules, $\approx 2.2$
M params in learned stages); Lambda x CFM coupling adapter (2-layer
numpy MLP, 64-d output); variance MCTS node structure (wrapper over
R17 \texttt{MCTSProofSearch}, no new learnable parameters);
Hilbert-ASTSR composite (3-stage module, $\approx 0.15$ M reward
head).  Each is CPU-only on the math skeleton layer and ships with
a W2.x adapter for the live pipeline.  Total shipped parameter
count is $\approx 2.4$ M for learned stages, plus the R17
\texttt{LipmanFlowMatchingAdapter} ($0.83$ M) which is
do-not-modify as of W2.1.
